Thank you for joining us for TxGIS Day, below is information about each day of the event, across each of the in-person events happening in the state

Texas A&M University TxGIS Day

Parking Validations

  • If you are not affiliated with the university you may be able to pick up a 1-day parking pass (each day) from the TxGIS Day Check-In tables which will be present outside of each TxGIS Day session room.
  • The number of these passes may be limited, so please grab one early if free parking is important to you.

Monday, November 17th

  • The majority of today's events are scheduled at the Student Computing Center (SCC) located on TAMU Main Campus.
  • Plentiful parking is available at any of the TAMU parking garages for TxGIS Day attendees and guests. The closest parking garage to the SCC is the Central Campus Garage (CCG).

Tuesday, November 18th

  • The majority of today's events are scheduled at the Texas A&M Memorial Student Center (MSC) Gates Ballroom, Room 2400 located on TAMU Main Campus at 275 Joe Routt Blvd, College Station, TX 77843.

Career Fair Booth Setup, Material Storage & Return Shipping

  • Setup: Event sponsors may begin setting up their booths (table cloths, popup signs, etc.) 30 minutes prior to the Career & Resources Fair event.
  • Storage: Event sponsors may store their materials at the TxGIS Day Check-In tables which will be located in the MSC outside of each of the TxGIS Day sessions.
  • Return Shipping: Return shipping is simple. Just bring a return shipping label to affix to your materials, and we will take care of the rest.
