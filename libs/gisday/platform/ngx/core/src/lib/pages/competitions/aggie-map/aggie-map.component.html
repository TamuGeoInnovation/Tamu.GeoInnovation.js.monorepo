New this year, TxGIS Day will hold an Aggie Map Design competition. We are looking for students to improve any and all aspects of the Aggie Map experience.

  • Better cartography
  • Better symbology
  • Better color choices
  • Alternative designs for visually impaired users
  • Better screen positioning of elements across mobile and/or desktop
  • Better user flow for multi-step maps such as Football Parking

This event will be judged by a panel of audience members and experts in the field.

This will be a single combined competition for both graduate and undergraduate students at Texas A&M University.

We invite all students from Texas A&M University to submit their Aggie Map Design ideas for the future of Aggie Map.

Participants will have the opportunity to win prizes (best team or individual submission).

How to Create An Aggie Map Design Submission

  • Review current the Aggie Map Application, along with the cartography used on special events such as Football Parking, Campus Move In, Twelfth Man Parking Maps & Transportation Services Maps, and other TAMU Transportation Maps.
  • If doing a cartography redesign: load the TAMU GIS data into your ArcGIS Online, ArcGIS Pro, or other mapping & cartography software including PowerPoint or Paint.
    • Basemap: https://gis.tamu.edu/arcgis/rest/services/FCOR/TAMU_BaseMap/MapServer
    • Transportation layers: https://gis.tamu.edu/arcgis/rest/services/TS
    • Use your cartographic expertise to make a better looking map.
  • If doing an interface redesign, use one of the available mock-up tools to build out a new user interface/flow such as Canva or Figma
    • Use your software design expertise to propose a new UI/UX, layouts, or user flow for multi-step maps.
  • Write an abstract of why your design work is a step up from what we currently have.

How to Submit Your Aggie Map Design Work

  • Create an log into your TxGIS Day Account.
  • Complete your TxGIS Day profile.
  • Create your design work using any editing software: PowerPoint, Illustrator, ArcGIS Online, Canva, Figma, Paint.
  • Turn your design work into an image by taking a screenshot or saving it as a PDF file.
  • Place the PDF file of your poster into a publicly accessible location such as your Google Drive folder.
  • Ensure that your PDF file is publicly available by attempting to browse to the link via an Incognito browser window.
  • Submit your design competition application through the submission section of the TxGIS Day website within your account.

Competition Rules

  • Any cartography, user interface, or user experience work will be accepted - static images of screenshots, interactive web maps or story maps, demos of improved website UI/UX, and more will all be accepted.
  • Submission may be proposing an improvement to any aspect of Aggie Map - alternative design element layouts, refactored mobile or desktop versions, new symbolization, new color choices, re-arrangements of mobile menus, repositioning of interface elements, new user flows for multi-step maps like Football Parking.
  • Submissions may be for individual or team work.
  • You may submit unlimited entries per person.
  • Submissions must be original - unique work.
  • Submission files may not exceed 10MB.
  • Winners do not have to be present at the awards announcement to win but maintain the responsibility of collecting any prizes that are won in the contest.
  • Winners will be responsible for any government taxes.
  • Only individuals and/or teams selected as finalists will present in the Aggie Map competition session.
  • Prizes - 1st, 2nd and 3rd place prizes will be awarded.
  • Only one (1) prize can be awarded per entry (even if an entry has multiple authors, only one prize is awarded).

Judging

All finalists and the winner will be selected by a panel of faculty and industry guests.

  • Finalists selection criteria: Contribution to improving Aggie Map.
  • Overall winner: Quality of design work and oral presentation.
