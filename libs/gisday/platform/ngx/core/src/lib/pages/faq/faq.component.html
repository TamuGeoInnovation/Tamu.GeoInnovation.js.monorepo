Frequently Asked Questions

What you need to know

Should I use my University account username and password to log into the website?

No. The {{ site.name }} website is completely separate from Texas A&M's Howdy system. If you have never signed up before on the {{ site.name }} website, you should create an account.

Are all of the {{ site.name }} events free?

Yes. There is no cost to attend any of the {{ site.name }} events.

I am not a student, faculty, or staff member at a university or college - can I come to {{ site.name }}?

Yes. We strongly encourage members of the community, colleagues and students from other universities, people from industry, and folks from local, state, and federal agencies to attend.

I am not a student, faculty, or staff member at a university or college - are all the events really free?

Yes. Even if you are not a faculty, staff, or student at a college or university, all {{ site.name }} events are free.

Who is allowed to participate in the competitions? Students, Faculty, Staff, Non-Academic people?

The {{ site.name }} competitions are open only to current Texas A&M students, graduate and undergraduate.

How do I check in using the {{ site.name }} website?

You can check in to a session on the {{ site.name }} website from shortly before it starts until shortly after it ends: usually from 5 minutes before it starts until 10 minutes after it ends. Some sessions allow check-in at any time. Follow these steps:

  • Create an account on the {{ site.name }} website, and make sure that you are logged into your account.
  • Complete your Profile. You cannot check in until it is complete.
  • Find the session you are attending and open its details page.
  • For an in-person session, click Checkin on the session's page and allow your phone or computer to share your location: you need to be at the session's building. If your phone will not share its location, scan the session's QR code, shown on the screen in the room, instead.
  • For a hybrid or online session, scan the session's QR code, shown on the presenter's slides or shared screen, with your phone. The page it opens checks you in and says so.

How do I certify my attendance using the {{ site.name }} website?

You can certify your attendance at any of the {{ site.name }} events using the {{ site.name }} website. Follow these steps:

  • Create an account on the {{ site.name }} website.
  • Make sure that you are logged into your account on the {{ site.name }} website.
  • Open the Classes page (from the Account menu, choose Profile, then Classes in the page's left menu) and select all of the classes that you want to notify professors for.
  • From 5 minutes before the session starts until 10 minutes after it ends, follow the check-in instructions to check in.

Is there a {{ site.name }} app for Android or iOS?

There are no longer apps for GIS Day in the Google Play or Apple App stores. They are no longer needed, since all functions of the site work on mobile devices, including the check-in process and the data collection competition.

If you have previously installed a version of the GIS Day app for Android or iOS, you can safely uninstall it.

Where do I find the Zoom link for the events?

The Zoom information for each online or hybrid session is on its details page. Follow these steps:

  • Create an account on the {{ site.name }} website.
  • Make sure that you are logged into your account on the {{ site.name }} website.
  • Find the session you want to attend and open its details page.
  • Open the Online Broadcast section, where you will find the Zoom link and its meeting details. It shows them only when you are logged in.

Will the videos be available after the event?

Some videos will be available at the GIS Day YouTube channel.
