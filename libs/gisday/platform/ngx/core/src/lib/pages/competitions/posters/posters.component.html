Each year, TxGIS Day hosts a geospatial student research poster exhibition and competition. This event is judged by a panel of audience members and experts in the field.

This year, there will be a single combined competition for both graduate and undergraduate students at Texas A&M University.

We invite all students from Texas A&M University to submit their geospatial work. Participants will have the opportunity to win prizes (best team or individual submission).

Submit Your Poster

Prizes

Prizes for the GIS Poster Competition are being generously provided by the Texas A&M Center for Teaching Excellence, through a Montague-CTE Scholar Award won by Dr. Heng Cai, Assistant Professor, Texas A&M Department of Geography.

  • 1st prize: $250
  • 2nd prize: $150
  • 3rd prize: $100

Registration and Poster Submission

  • Due Date: Monday November 10th, 2025
  • Time: 5:00PM Central Time
  • Location: Online submission

How to Submit a Poster

  • Create an log into your TxGIS Day Account.
  • Complete your TxGIS Day profile.
  • Create your poster using any visual editing software: PowerPoint, Illustrator, ArcGIS Online.
  • Save your poster file as a PDF file.
  • Place the PDF file of your poster into a publicly accessible location such as your Google Drive folder.
  • Ensure that your PDF file is publicly available by attempting to browse to the link via an Incognito browser window.
  • Submit your poster competition application though the submission section of the TxGIS Day website within your account.

Eligibility

Only current graduate and undergraduate students are eligible to participate in this competition.

Finalist Selection

  • Finalists will be notified on Wednesday November 12th, 2025
  • Finalists will be expected to print and bring their poster for display during the Poster Competition Session (Tuesday November 18th)
  • Only finalists will be allowed to show their posters at the Poster Competition Session

Presentation Competition Session

  • Date: Tuesday November 18th, 2025
  • Time: 4:00-5:00PM
  • Location: In person - TAMU Memorial Student Center, Gates Ballroom 2400
  • Finalists must be present during the poster session in order to be considered for awards.

Awards Announcement

Winners will be announced on Wednesday, November 19th at during the lunch session.

Competition Rules

  • Printed posters cannot exceed 48 inches wide by 36 inches tall.
  • Students are responsible for printing their own poster.
  • Posters can be printed at the TAMU Student Computing Center (SCC). View SCC printing services on their webpage.
  • Any GIS and/or Remote Sensing work will be accepted.
  • Submission may be of ongoing or completed work.
  • Submissions may be for individual or team work.
  • You may submit unlimited entries per person.
  • Submissions must be original - unique work.
  • Poster submissions must be in pdf format.
  • Poster submission may not to exceed 10MB.
  • Winners do not have to be present at the awards announcement to win but maintain the responsibility of collecting any prizes that are won in the contest.
  • Winners will be responsible for any government taxes.
  • Only individuals and/or teams selected as finalists will present in the poster competition session.
  • Prizes - 1st, 2nd and 3rd place prizes will be awarded.
  • Only one (1) prize can be awarded per entry (even if an entry has multiple authors, only one prize is awarded).

Judging

  • All finalists and the winner will be selected by a panel of faculty and industry guests.
  • Finalists selection criteria: Contribution to or use of GIS as presented on poster & visual appeal of poster.
  • Overall winner: Quality of poster and oral presentation.
