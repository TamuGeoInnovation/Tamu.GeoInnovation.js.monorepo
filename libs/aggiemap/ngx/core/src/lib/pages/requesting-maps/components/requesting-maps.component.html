Requesting New Aggie Maps & Changes to Existing Maps

The Texas A&M Aggie Map provides an interactive mapping platform for Texas A&M groups and organizations to inform the public of important information related to transportation and parking for events and activities on the Texas A&M campus.

The Aggie Map Team invites you to request information about your event be added as a special Campus Event Map on Aggie Map to help spread the word about your event and to support the communication of parking and transportation information for your event.

Please be aware of the following processes, deadlines, and expectations the team requires in order to ensure that your event information can be added to Aggie Map in a timely manner, and to allow for proper testing to maintain overall stable Aggie Map operations.

Requesting New Aggie Maps

The Aggie Map Team wants to help support your event be a success!

Please fill out the Aggie Map Request Form to start your map creation journey.

The team will review your request to determine if it aligns with the purpose of Aggie Map and if the Aggie Map platform can accommodate your Aggie Map.

The decision to add or not add your map to Aggie Map will ultimately be made by the Texas A&M GIS Steering Committee. Not all map requests will be approved.

If approved, the team will work with you to obtain your data or to provide a method for you to create your data yourself. You, the owner of the map, are responsible for ensuring that your map's data are up-to-date, accurate, and remain maintained.

The team may request additional context or data changes from you, and will eventually generate and publish a development version of your map for comment and review.

Once approved and completed, the map will go live onto Aggie Map. You will be able to share the link to your Aggie Map with your attendees, guests, and your other communication channels.

Design Notes

The Aggie Map Team is responsible for ensuring that all Aggie Maps share consistent layouts, look-and-feel, functionality, cartography, and symbology, and that all maps are consistent with Texas A&M branding and standards.

The team may not be able to accommodate all desired design or functionality choices for each individual map if they conflict with Aggie Map design standards and branding.

Timelines & Expectations

The Aggie Map Team performs detailed and extensive usability and load testing of all new and existing maps on a continuous basis. This work requires significant effort by multiple people across multiple days.

The team requires an appropriate amount of time in advance of the launch of your map to design, build, and test all maps before they can be deployed into production as Aggie Maps.

The team strives to have all new Aggie Maps finalized and published to production a minimum of two weeks before when they are needed to support an event.

As such, please be aware of the following deadlines:

  1. Map requests must be made a minimum of 30 days in advance of when the map should be published.
  2. Final changes to maps, data, functionality, and/or design must be made a minimum of two weeks in advance of when the map should be published.
  3. Maps will be locked seven days before the event in order to provide ample time for load testing and other infrastructure verifications.

Last minute changes to the design, layout, data, or functionality of new or existing Aggie Maps — i.e., changes requested less than two weeks before your event — cannot typically be accommodated and should not be expected.
