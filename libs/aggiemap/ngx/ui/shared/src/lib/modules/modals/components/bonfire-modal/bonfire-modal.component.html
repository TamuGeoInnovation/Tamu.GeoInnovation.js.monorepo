Honoring the 12: 25th Anniversary of the Aggie Bonfire Collapse

The Bonfire Memorial celebrates the tradition, history and camaraderie of Texas A&M and the dedication of those involved in the tragic 1999 collapse. Uniting Aggies past, present and future, the memorial is comprised of the Tradition Plaza, the History Walk, the Spirit Ring and the 12 portals.

Join us in commemorating the 25th anniversary by exploring this tribute on your digital device and preserving the memory of those we honor.
