13-0 Burn

The 13-0 Burn is a nod to a rivalry event dating back to 1920. This year, we are firing up the grills and serving up mouthwatering BBQ in a cook-off to celebrate our Aggie pride with unmatched enthusiasm. Whether you're a seasoned fan or new to the rivalry, this event is all about savoring great food and embracing the heart of the Aggie Spirit. Sign up a team to compete in the cook off!
