## Why the tooling missed it

The look-first list in CLAUDE.md names components, services, helpers, styles and smoke helpers. It has nothing for **a behaviour declared in a definition file**, so a map's author copies the nearest map (the #2587 gap). The lint and test tooling checks that each file is correct, not that two files agree, so even the drifted swatch (#2601) and the four ignored `isDevelopmentHostname` callers pass everything. The smoke suite has its own standalone `tsconfig`, which may be why it re-declares what the app exports (`MAP_PROBE_GLOBAL`), though it already imports from `libs/` for other things.
