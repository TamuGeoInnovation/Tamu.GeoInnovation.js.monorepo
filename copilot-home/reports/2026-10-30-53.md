## What changed

1. **The icons render**, in their normal place and order, inactive. `HIDDEN_PLANNER_MODES` is now `UNAVAILABLE_PLANNER_MODES = [3, 4, 5, 6, 8]`, one list; its comment says this is a judgement about the answers (impedance `Length`, no bus schedule), not a limit of the network, that a greyed Driving says "not yet" where no Driving says "we do not do this", and that emptying the list brings them back.
2. **The one question became two.** `offeredTravelMode` is gone. *May it be chosen and solved?* is `selectableTravelMode(supported, mode)` and the solver (`getTravelModeFromRule`) uses it, so an unavailable mode never reaches a solve. *Does it appear?* has no function: every toggle appears. `travelTypeUnavailable(modes)` says a button is drawn disabled (every mode in it unavailable).
3. **The toggle is shown again**; `travelTypeOffered`, which drew nothing, is deleted. Driving [3,4,8] and Bus [5,6] render disabled in the desktop and the phone picker.
4. **Disabled in fact.** The toggle is a `div role="button"`, which has no `disabled` of its own, so: `aria-disabled="true"`, `tabindex="-1"` (out of the tab order), the `disabled` class (grey, `not-allowed` cursor, from the existing partial), and `setTravelMode()` returns at once, so click, Enter and Space do nothing. ETA placeholders are not drawn on a disabled toggle. Walking and Biking are unchanged (`tabindex="0"`, live).
5. **Why, where a person can see it.** `title` and `aria-label` say it. **Proposed wording: "Driving directions are not available yet" / "Bus directions are not available yet"** (`unavailableTravelModeTitle(name)`, built from the toggle's name; the aria-label is "Routing Mode: Driving, Driving directions are not available yet"). One function, so the maintainer's own words are a one-line change.
6. **`?mode=5` fallback stays** (5 to walking; 4 and 6 to accessible walking, 3 and 8 to walking). The panel now shows Bus greyed out, so the fallback is visible.
7. **PR 2577 check** (`test/smoke/aggiemap/directions.spec.ts`) keeps both lists. The second is `CHOSEN_ON_DEVELOPMENT_NETWORK = [1, 2, 7]`, its test and message say "chosen", and it uses `selectableTravelMode` and `UNAVAILABLE_PLANNER_MODES` in the comments. The network list `[1,2,3,5,7,8]` and the "solves a route in every mode the network lists" test are untouched.

Unchanged from batch 52: modes 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 (leaving 1, 2, 7), red first, `scripts/check-in-volume.sh <branch> maps-feature-trip-planner,aggiemap-angular` exit 0.

The release note is renamed `docs/releases/unreleased/2580-disable-car-bus-modes.md` (and the screenshot folder `docs/screenshots/2580-disable-car-bus-modes/`), reworded: *What to test on dev* now says Driving and Bus are visible but greyed out, cannot be clicked or tabbed to, say "not available yet" on hover, and that `mode=5` opens as walking with Bus greyed out.
