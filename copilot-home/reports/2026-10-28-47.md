## What I did

Read the code first. The panel is shared the way the issue says, but the issue's file paths are stale: the popup is `tamu-gisc-feature-popup` in `libs/maps/feature/popup`, drawn inside the sidebar (`libs/aggiemap/ngx/ui/desktop` and the events sidebar) on top of the routed tab view, and the basemap selector is the **Settings** tab's `tamu-gisc-basemap-gallery`.

**#1540 is the same fault as #1806** ("Sidebar controls do nothing while a feature's details are open", closed). PR 1813 (`c78fce58e`) fixed it in `RevealSidebarOnPopupDirective`: a click on a `tamu-gisc-sidebar-tab` while a popup shows closes the popup and keeps the panel open. On the question the issue poses: it was **panel ownership, not pointer events** - the tab only changed the route under the popup (the URL said `/settings` and the details stayed in front). The fix has a unit test (`reveal-sidebar.tabs.spec.ts`).
