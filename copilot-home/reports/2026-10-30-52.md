## What changed

- `trip-planner-travel-modes.ts`: one list, `HIDDEN_PLANNER_MODES = [3, 4, 5, 6, 8]`, with the reason beside it: **hidden, not a limit of the network** (Bike, Bus and Drive use impedance `Length`; no bus schedule). `offeredTravelMode` returns false for them, so the planner neither offers nor solves them (Drive and Bus no longer appear among the results). Reversing the list is the only change needed to bring them back.
- The mode toggle draws nothing when all its modes are hidden (`travelTypeOffered`), in both the desktop and the mobile picker.
- **Settled:**
  1. Modes hidden: 3 drive with parking pass, 4 accessible drive, 5 bus, 6 accessible bus, 8 visitor drive. **1 walk, 2 accessible walk and 7 bike remain.**
  2. A link naming a hidden mode (`?mode=5`) opens as walking (`modeOrFallback`); the accessible ones (4, 6) open as accessible walking (2), so the intent survives. Chosen over an empty panel or an error because a shared link should still show a trip. Live check: `mode=5` on the branch gave Walking active, Walk 35 min and Bike 35 min with steps, 2 solves (not 4).
  3. The PR 2577 check in `test/smoke/aggiemap/directions.spec.ts` is kept as an alarm and now asserts two lists: the modes the network has for the planner (`[1,2,3,5,7,8]`, still catches drift such as Drive_ADA appearing or Bus dropping) and the modes offered (`[1,2,7]`). The "solves a route in every mode the network lists" test is unchanged, so Drive and Bus are still proven to solve on dev.
- Release note `docs/releases/unreleased/2580-hide-car-bus-modes.md`; its *What to test on dev* row names Walking, Biking and accessible walking, plus the `mode=5` link.
