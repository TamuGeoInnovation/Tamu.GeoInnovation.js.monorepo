### #2079: sub-admins

Recommends **within one site, keyed on the place** (the admin pages' "Organizations"), because per site cannot work for
txgisday.org and gisday.tamu.edu, which share one database. Ten decisions at the end. Points worth knowing before
reading it:

- **The roles already exist and are half-wired.** `GIS Day Organizer` and `GIS Day Manager` are in Auth0 and in
  `GISDayRoles`, and the admin route already admits both, but neither holds a permission, so every save they try is
  refused. The design gives Organizer the editing permissions and adds one admin-only permission,
  `manage:all-organizations`, as the override, using the existing `isOwnerOrPermitted` pattern.
- **Speakers and sponsors have no organization today.** It proposes an owner `place` column on each, backfilled from
  their events, as #3048 did for broadcasts.
- **A trap found on the way:** gisday.tamu.edu picks its sessions by place **name** (`sessionsFrom`). If an organizer
  could rename their place, Texas A&M's sessions would silently vanish from gisday.tamu.edu. The design keeps names
  admin-only (decision 7). Nothing is broken today, so no issue was filed.

### #2072: landing page

Recommends **the same application, txgisday.org's own homepage, no new mode**: organization cards (logo, name, its
dates, its session count, one link: its own site, else its sessions on txgisday.org, else its website), with `tamu` and
`homeLinks` turned off on txgisday.org so Texas A&M's content stays only on gisday.tamu.edu. Every statewide link keeps
working; only `/highschool` is sent on to gisday.tamu.edu. Accounts unchanged. Six decisions at the end. Most of it
exists already: the homepage's Participating Organizations section and the Sessions filter by organization.
