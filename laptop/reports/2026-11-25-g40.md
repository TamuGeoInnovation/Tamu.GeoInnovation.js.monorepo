## How "stale" was checked

After each action the script sent **no further input at all**. It moved the mouse away, waited 700 ms, then read the page. That is well
past one render, and it is the zoneless failure itself: a view that catches up only on the next click or keypress would fail the read.

**Each check asked for the specific thing that must have changed**, not just "something changed":

- `aria-checked` on the option clicked;
- the radio icon's glyph;
- the slide toggle's highlight position;
- the file name shown or cleared;
- the modal's heading and its season list;
- the tab marked `aria-selected`, **and** the tab panel's content swapped.

Script: `laptop-tools/g40-zoneless.cjs`, a local file.
