## The fix, and what the footer shows without a season

`FooterComponent`'s Past Years were `seasons.filter((year) => year < active.year)`, with `active` null when no season is active.

**With no active season they are now the seasons before this year, newest first.** That is my choice, as asked:
- "past" keeps meaning past;
- a site between seasons still links its earlier GIS Days;
- a site with no seasons at all lists none.

With a season active, nothing changes.
