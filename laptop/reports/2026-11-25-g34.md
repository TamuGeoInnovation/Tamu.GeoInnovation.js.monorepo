## How

- **The stack:** the local GIS Day stack (my helper around `scripts/gisday-local.sh`), on the scratch database's made-up records, signed in as the
  txgisday-dev test admin. Both captures were read-only (every non-GET blocked).
- **The data:** the designer opens the active season's form, here the **made-up** 2026 one (`vgi_forms`
  `b4000000-0000-4000-8000-00000000f001`, 487 characters, two made-up questions). I appended one question to its `model` with no `options` key:
  `{"attribute":"made_up_no_options","title":"Made-up question saved without its options","instructions":"","enabled":true,"isDiscriminator":false,"type":"select"}`.
- **One thing learned on the way:** my first version of that question also lacked `isDiscriminator`. The branch then rendered it but logged
  `NG01055: Cannot find control with path: 'isDiscriminator'`, which was **my test data's fault, not the branch's**: every real question carries
  that key. With it added, only the options were missing, as #3614 describes, and both images were taken again with that data.
- **Undone:** the question was removed by replacing its exact text, and the model is back to **487 characters with no trace of it**
  (`CHARINDEX` 0). A first backup to a file was cut short by `sqlcmd`'s display width, so I did not rely on it. The stack is stopped.
