### (b) Even with routing working, a visitor's drive never runs the visitor-lot query

Read in `trip-planner.service.ts` and `parking.service.ts` at `a923432c4`:

- **Only one place calls `getAuthorizedParkingLocations()`**, at `trip-planner.service.ts:1034`, and it runs only when the mode has
  `determinants.parking_pass`. Only modes 3 and 4 ("Use Parking Pass") have that.
- **A visitor's drive is mode 8** ("Visitor Accessible" or "Visitor Non-Accessible", with determinants `{ accessible }` only). It goes straight
  to `else`, and the route is solved to the destination with no lot lookup at all.
- **In modes 3 and 4, `parking_pass` is true**, and its `set` effect writes `Use_Permit: true`. So the `visitor-parking` entry,
  `include: Use_Permit === false`, is left out.

**So no path in today's UI builds the `visitor-parking` query, before or after the fix.** That is why the issue's visible result (*"a
visitor with no permit was offered no lots"*) cannot be photographed: a visitor is offered no lots on either side of the fix, because no lots are
looked up for a visitor at all. The fix is still right for the query it changes, and the unit tests prove that. It just changes nothing a visitor
sees, which CLAUDE.md's *"Ask the service before you change a definition"* warns about, and the pull request should say so rather than show a
before/after.
