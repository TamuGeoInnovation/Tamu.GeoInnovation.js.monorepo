## What this changes

**The phone's directions, and their actions, now render the desktop's templates.** Each had a phone template of its own beside the
desktop's. The two actions templates were byte-identical. The two directions templates had drifted: the phone's always drew its actions, and
had no "Calculating route" or "No reasonable route was found!" feedback.

- **`trip-planner-directions`** gains a `layout`, `'desktop'` or `'mobile'`. The mobile layout adds only two things to the shared template:
  the bottom sheet's drag handle, and the phone's actions.
- **`trip-planner-directions-actions`**: the phone's component renders the base template. Its stylesheet stays, because its two lighter
  borders are a real difference, not drift.
- **Both phone classes stay.** The bottom sheet hosts its drag directive on `tamu-gisc-trip-planner-directions-mobile`, and each class carries
  its stylesheet. So `trip-planner-bottom`, which is in open PR 3653, is untouched.
- **Both phone templates are deleted.**

### What a phone shows: unchanged, and why

**The bottom sheet exists only for a found route**: `trip-planner-bottom` draws it when `result()?.isFulfilled && !result().isError`. For a
found route, both templates drew the same thing: the overview, the actions and the steps. The states that drifted are calculating and
no-route, and there the phone shows no sheet at all, before and after. Its only signal is the top bar's "--" against the mode.

So a phone user sees no difference. The shared template would now give them the desktop's feedback, but only once the sheet is shown in
those states, and that is `trip-planner-bottom`'s gate, not this change.
