## Two faults already on `development`, filed (Low)

- **[#3635](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/3635): RSVP types and submission types cannot be saved.**
  - **The cause:** `rsvp-type.controller.ts` and `submission-type.controller.ts` answer `NotImplementedException` (501) for `@Post()` and
    `@Patch(':guid')`, while the admin offers Add and Update.
  - **What happens:** every save shows the 501 toast.
  - **The fix is a decision:** implement the two routes, or take Add and Update off the two admin pages.
- **[#3636](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/3636): the initial survey page throws and shows both headings.**
  - **The error:** `templateRef.createEmbeddedViewImpl is not a function`, from `<ng-template [ngTemplateOutlet]="noFeedback">` given
    `#noFeedback`, which is on a `<div>`, not a template.
  - **What shows:** "Pre-Event…" and "Event User Impact Survey" both.
  - **Not checked:** whether its questions render with real data. The scratch database may have none.

Both: Bug, `bug`, GIS Day Angular + NestJS as Todo. Images are on this branch:
- `laptop/reports/2026-11-25-g32-rsvp-type-501.png`;
- `laptop/reports/2026-11-25-g32-initial-survey.png`.
