## What this changes

<!-- What a reader needs to know. Why, not only what. -->

The Check-ins dashboard (#3006) shipped on the RSVP dashboard's pattern. #2893's decisions of 13 November (5A) asked for more on each event's row and in its list of people. This adds what was missing, except the chart (below).

- **Walk-ins:** each event's check-ins by someone with no RSVP to that event. An RSVP to another event doesn't count. The API compares each check-in's account with the event's RSVPs.
- **Showed up:** the share of an event's RSVPs who checked in, that is, check-ins minus walk-ins over RSVPs. It shows as a whole percent, and as "–" for an event nobody RSVP'd to. The total row gives the season's rate (the sums' share), not a sum of percents.
- **Outside the session's time:** in an event's list, a check-in made outside its session's time (#2635, the `outsideSessionTime` the API already records) says so under its time. The CSV has an `Outside session time` column, "yes" for those people.

**How**
- **The shared table** gains a rate column (`percentOf`). Each cell holds the share itself, so the column sorts by rate, with "–" last, and the total row computes the sums' share. Count columns are unchanged.
- **The shared people list** shows the mark, and the CSV gains its column, only when the people carry `outsideSessionTime`. So the RSVP and VGI dashboards are unchanged.
- **The API's `EventDashboard`** hands its season records to the check-in dashboard (`season()`) rather than reading the check-ins twice. Its new `person()` hook adds the mark.

**Not built: the chart.** #2893 decided on check-ins by hour across GIS Day week. #3006 shipped a running total by day with each day's bars. The year-against-year comparison (#3215) was built on that chart since. Switching it to hours means reworking the chart all the dashboards share, so it waits for a decision.

With the two new columns, the table (12 columns) no longer fits beside the admin menu at 1280 px. It scrolls sideways in its own box, as it already does on a narrower screen (#3029).
