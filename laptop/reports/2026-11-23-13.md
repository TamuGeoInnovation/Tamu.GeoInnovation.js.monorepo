## What it does

Under **Check-ins over time**, a second chart, **Check-ins by hour**, counts check-ins in each hour of the days the season's
events run, in Central time. Opening an event adds that event's line, as on the chart by day. The chart by day and the
year comparison are unchanged.

- **Reused, not a second chart:** the shared `tamu-gisc-line-chart` (`ui-kits-ngx-charts`) gains an `interval` input,
  `'day'` (the default, so the four other dashboards are unchanged) or `'hour'`. `countsByHourOver()` sits beside `countsByDay()`.
  The page's series come from `dashboardHourly()`, beside `dashboardSeries()`.
- **Site filter:** no API change was needed. The season dashboard's `made` list already holds only the records of the events
  the site lists (#3024), so the hourly chart uses the same filter as the rest of the page.
- **Which days:** every hour from midnight on the first event day to the end of the last, in Central time. Check-ins on other
  days are left out. A stray event day far from the week (2023 lists one on 30 November) would widen the axis. That is
  noted, not handled.

## What this changes

<!-- What a reader needs to know. Why, not only what. -->

#2893 decided on a chart of check-ins by hour, because check-ins happen only during GIS Day week, so a chart by day shows a few steps and little else. #3006 shipped the chart by day. The maintainer decided on 23 November to keep it and add an hourly chart under it (#3314). This adds that chart.

- **Check-ins by hour:** every hour of the days the season's events run, in Central time, with an hour's count on hover and drag-to-zoom as on the chart above. Opening an event adds its own line. Check-ins made on other days are left out.
- **Same site filter:** the API's season dashboard already lists only the site's events and their records (#3024), and the chart is drawn from those records.
- **One chart, not two:** the shared line chart (`ui-kits-ngx-charts`) takes an `interval`, `'day'` by default or `'hour'`. By the hour, its axis, labels and hover read in hours, and `countsByHourOver()` counts the hours. The other dashboards draw by day as before.

**Stacked on #2893's branch** (`feat/2893-check-ins-dashboard-rest`), which had not merged when this was built. This pull request's own changes are its last five commits.
