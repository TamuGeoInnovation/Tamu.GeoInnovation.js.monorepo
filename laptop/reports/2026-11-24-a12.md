## Summary for the decision

- **The one regression:** `NearmapCSBasemap` in #3352's scraper check. It is deterministic, and every release tier will fail on it until the
  check, or how that basemap names its service, changes. It is a fault in the check; visitors see nothing different.
- **Real on a quiet machine, and already known:**
  - indoor-track-parking's layer order (2 of 3 loads);
  - break-summer's Lot 100g popup.
- **Flaky only:** the rest of the eight, the dining-service and basemap-service hiccups, and timed-parking.

**Also noticed, not filed:** `NearmapCSBasemap`'s URL in `libs/maps/feature/basemap/src/lib/shared/basemaps.definition.ts` carries
Nearmap's API key in its path, in this public repository, since #415. A browser needs the key to fetch the tiles, so it is probably known,
but worth a look. The key is not repeated here.
