## What I found

**There is already a check that names the network's `Drive` mode and asserts a route came back.** It is in `directions.spec.ts:362`, *solves a
route between two campus points in every mode it lists*. It reads `supportedTravelModes`, sends each mode as `travelMode`, and requires
`routes.features` to be non-empty. That is exactly what steps 1 and 2 ask for.

**It is green on production, correctly.** Its two points are on University Dr, and Drive solves between them: 7.0 min, asked today.

So I asked why my a43 pairs failed. The service's own `Drive` mode, named explicitly, from University Dr (-96.3300, 30.6150):

| Destination | Drive | Walk | Drive without `No Drive` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Central Campus Garage | **2.7 min** | 12.7 | 9.3 |
| University Center Garage | **4.7** | 15.1 | 12.2 |
| West Campus Garage | **5.3** | 24.1 | 15.6 |
| Polo Rd Garage | **6.0** | 23.4 | 12.2 |
| Northside Garage | **6.4** | 22.6 | 11.0 |

The garage coordinates are the centroids of the six `Garage Visitor` lots in the production Parking Lots layer.

**Drive reaches every visitor garage and every public road.** It fails only on points inside the pedestrian core, which only footpaths reach.
That is what `No Drive` is for. Dropping it "solves" those trips by driving down footpaths, which is why every such solve is *slower*. Your
restriction table reads the same way: the pair you tested ends in the core.

**The production app agrees.** https://aggiemap.tamu.edu/map/d/trip?mode=8&stops=@30.6150,-96.3300@30.61655,-96.33769, a visitor's drive to
the Central Campus Garage, shows **3 minutes, 0.63 miles**, with turn-by-turn directions.
