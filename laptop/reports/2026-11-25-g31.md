## What the admin page actually does

The run used the local stack from `development` at `8f81e205d`, signed in as the txgisday-dev test admin, on the scratch database's made-up records.
The only write let through was the one `POST /api/seasons`; none were blocked.

- **"Add season" sends no year.** `createSeason()` calls `createEntity()` empty, and `POST /api/seasons` runs **`SeasonService.clonePreviousSeason()`**:
  - it copies the latest season by year into year + 1;
  - it deactivates that latest season if it was active;
  - it makes the copy **active**.
- **`SeasonService.create()`, which #973 fixed, has no caller** outside its unit test. No page sends a year, and the edit form has no "add" mode.

So #980's three expectations do not hold, and none of them is a bug:
- a year cannot be chosen;
- the new season is active, not inactive (that is the clone, by design);
- a repeat click creates the next year again and never answers 409.

#973's `create()` is proven only by its unit test. Whether #980 should be closed, or rewritten against the clone, is Q-g31-1.
