### The football map is called the Football Gameday Transportation Map

Its listing in All Maps and the title of its first page say **Football Gameday Transportation Map**, and its
description is "Plan your trip to campus for football gameday." It was "Football" in the listing and "Football
Transportation" on the page. The game-day notification on the main map uses the new name too. The short name stays
where a sentence needs it, so the schedule link still reads "Click here for Football details".
([#445](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/445))
