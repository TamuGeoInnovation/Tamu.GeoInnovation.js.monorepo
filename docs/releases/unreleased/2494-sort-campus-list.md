### All Maps: the Campus Maps list is in alphabetical order

The Campus Maps section of the All Maps page listed the campuses in the order they were added, which
was fine with three campuses and is not with ten. It now lists them alphabetically by name, as the event
and parking lists already do
([#2494](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2494)).
