### The November 2026 campus basemap can be compared on dev

The GIS team published a new campus basemap on 19 November (`TAMU_BaseMap_20261119`). Dev's basemap gallery now
offers it as **Aggieland vector, November 2026**, beside the basemap the map opens on, so the two can be compared
before it is chosen for production. Production does not offer it, and the smoke suite fails if production ever asks
for it; when it moves is the maintainer's decision. A new smoke test switches to it and back again, the check that
was missing when a basemap change blanked the map on production (#2477, #2536).
([#3225](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/3225))
