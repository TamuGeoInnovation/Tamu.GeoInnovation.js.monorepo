### Fort Worth Law School joins the satellite campus maps

The GIS team published the services for the Texas A&M University School of Law campus in Fort Worth, and it is now one of the satellite campus maps
alongside Galveston, McAllen and DC / Bush School
([#2437](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2437)).

**It is listed on development only** for now, as the other satellite campuses were until
[#1490](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/1490) put them on
production. Nothing changes on production.
