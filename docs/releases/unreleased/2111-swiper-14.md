### The photo carousels in AggieMap's popups move to Swiper 14, for a critical advisory

The photo carousels in Points of Interest and Bonfire Memorial popups (on AggieMap and on the UES map) were built
on Swiper 11.2.10, which has a critical prototype-pollution advisory
([GHSA-hmx5-qpq5-p643](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-hmx5-qpq5-p643)). They now use Swiper 14.3.0. No code
changed: Swiper 14 keeps every option, event and import the popups use.

The one visible difference is the arrows. Swiper 14 makes their buttons square, so each arrow sits a few pixels
further in from the edge of the photo. Everything else in the images is unchanged, pixel for pixel, and in both
popups the carousel opens on its first photo and the next arrow moves it on, as before.
([#2111](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2111))
