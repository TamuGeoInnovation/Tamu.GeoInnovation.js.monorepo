### GIS Day: Texas A&M's site no longer shows three links under Explore Events

The homepage of [gisday.tamu.edu](https://gisday.tamu.edu) (and tamu.txgisday.org) had three links under the
Explore Events button: "Sign up for Career Cupid Speed Mentoring Events", "Nominate a TX GIS Hero" and "Watch
the how to get started video". They are gone from that site.

[txgisday.org](https://txgisday.org) keeps all three, and UCGIS's site keeps its how-to video. Nothing else
on any page changes ([#2431](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2431)).
