### GIS Day: the Monument Trail eligibility sentence gives the dates

The Monument Trail rules said only submissions "collected during the week prior to and the days before the awards
ceremony" would be considered for an award, which did not match the competition's real window. The sentence now
gives the dates: **only submissions made between Saturday, November 14 and 11:00 AM Central on Friday, November 20**
are considered
([#2497](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2497)).
