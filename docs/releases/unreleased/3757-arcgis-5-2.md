### The maps run ArcGIS 5.2.9

Every map now draws with ArcGIS 5.2.9, up from 5.1.27, and GIS Day's charts with amCharts 5.20.8, the version ArcGIS 5.2 needs, so the
two still share one copy. Nothing is meant to look different: the before and after below are the same map, drawn by each version.
([#3757](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/3757))
