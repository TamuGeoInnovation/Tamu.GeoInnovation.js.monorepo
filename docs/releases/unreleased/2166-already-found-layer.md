### GIS Day: the VGI competition map shows where memorials were already found last year

The competition map now shows, as open maroon rings, where monuments and memorial plaques were documented in
the 2025 competition, so participants can look elsewhere. They are the same finds the 2026 scoring counts as
already found: every 2025 submission that was not discarded, with 2025's statues and monuments shown as
monuments and its art left out. This year's submissions draw on top, as grey dots. The map shows only where
each was found and its type, nothing about who found it.
([#2166](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2166))
