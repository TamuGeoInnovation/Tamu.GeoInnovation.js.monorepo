### GIS Day: the VGI competition pages give the new dates

The VGI Competition page and the Monument Trail page said the competition starts on Monday, November 9 and that
winners are announced on Thursday, November 19. The competition now runs **Saturday, November 14 through Friday,
November 20, 2026**. Submissions count until **11:00 AM Central on Friday, November 20**, and the final winners are
announced **at the lunch session on Friday, November 20**. Both pages say so
([#2460](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2460)).
