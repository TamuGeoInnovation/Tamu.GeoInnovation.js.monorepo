### Four new satellite campuses join the campus maps

The GIS team published four more satellite campuses, and each is now one of the campus maps alongside Galveston, McAllen, DC / Bush School,
Fort Worth Law School and the Houston Space Institute:

- Mays City Center ([#2455](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2455))
- HSC Round Rock ([#2456](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2456))
- HSC Kingsville ([#2457](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2457))
- HSC Corpus Christi ([#2464](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2464))

**They are listed on development only** for now, as the Houston Space Institute is. Nothing changes on production.
