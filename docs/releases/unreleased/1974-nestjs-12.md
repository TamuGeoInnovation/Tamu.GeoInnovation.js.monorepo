### Not visible: NestJS 11 to 12

The NestJS APIs (UES Operations, GIS Day, OIDC, Mailroom and Geoservices) move from NestJS 11.2.7 to 12.2.0
([#1974](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/1974), the second of its two
steps), with every `@nestjs` package on 12 and Express 5.3. AggieMap and the event maps are Angular apps and do not
change.

NestJS 12 is published as ES modules only. The APIs stay CommonJS and load it through Node's `require(esm)`, which
needs Node 20.19 or later (22.12 or later on Node 22) wherever they run. `@nx/nest`, which only supplied code
generators, is removed because no Nx release accepts NestJS 12 yet.
