### Houston Space Institute joins the satellite campus maps

The GIS team published the services for the Texas A&M University Houston Space Institute, and it is now one of the satellite campus maps
alongside Galveston, McAllen and DC / Bush School
([#2438](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2438)).

**It is listed on development only** for now, as the other satellite campuses were until
[#1490](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/1490) put them on
production. Nothing changes on production.
