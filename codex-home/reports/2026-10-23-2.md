## Changes

Added and exported `DEFAULT_UPLOAD_LIMITS` from `libs/common/nest/services`:

- `fileSize`: 5 MiB
- `files`: 1
- `fields`: 50
- `fieldNameSize`: 100 bytes
- `fieldSize`: 1 MiB
- `parts`: 51

The GIS Day platform organization, place, speaker, and sponsor create/update routes use these defaults. One 5 MiB image suits the logo/photo use while bounding Multer's in-memory file buffer; the field and part ceilings also constrain multipart metadata.

Competition submissions preserve their existing contract of two photos up to 15 MiB each, and add the shared fields/name/field-size limits with `parts: 52` (50 text fields plus two files).

Both mailroom routes allow up to ten attachments at 5 MiB each (up to 50 MiB total), with the shared caps of 50 text fields, 100-byte field names, 1 MiB per text field, and 60 total parts. Ten files supports several attachments while bounding buffered input.
