## What this changes

<!-- What a reader needs to know. Why, not only what. -->

On txgisday.org the admin Events list holds every organization's sessions, a long list in which to find one organization's (#434).

**What changed**
- **An Organization choice** above the list. It offers the organizations whose venues hold the season's sessions, by name, after "Every organization", and keeps only the chosen one's sessions.
- **It is one of the list's own filters** (`listFilters`, `keepEntity`), beside the tags and slides filters. So the list base keeps it in the page's history (#2942), the "nothing shown" message and its Clear button cover it (#3049), and it works with the search.
- **The list base** now holds the season's records before its filters as `listed$`, so a filter can offer what they hold. Nothing else in the base changes.
- **The choices come from the sessions themselves,** not a second request. An organization with no sessions this season is not offered.
- **On gisday.tamu.edu,** "Show every organization's sessions" still decides whether other organizations' sessions are listed at all.
