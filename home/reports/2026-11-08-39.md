## 2. #2765: the sweep

**Where I stopped:** every one of the 200 registry entries was asked about, on both hosts. 98 were removed, 39 given a
reason, and **63 are left `unreviewed (#1523)`** because each needs a judgement rather than a measurement (listed below).

### How each removal was proven

1. **Both hosts asked, from the definitions' own URLs.** A throwaway spec dumped every titled layer with its URL as a
   production build and as a development build resolve it (pins included), and every URL was asked for its name on its
   host. **98 feature layers' titles equal, word for word, the name both production and development publish.**
2. **Exactly those 98 removed, and nothing else.** After editing, the dump was taken again on both builds and compared:
   98 titles gone, every one of them in the removable set, no layer vanished, no title added. A first pass that matched
   by text removed two same-named titles on other maps (Big Event's Traffic Flow, Argentina vs Honduras's Event Parking),
   which the comparison caught; those files were reverted. All 80 deleted lines are layer sources' `title:` (several
   generated layers share a line).
3. **In the app, on both hosts, with something that only exists when the layer resolved.** The branch built twice: as a
   development build at a dev host (development GIS server) and as the default build at `127.0.0.1` (production's).
   Every affected map opened, builder maps through the builder inventory's share links, and for each of the 98 layers the
   probe had to report **loaded, no error, the title shown equal to the old title, and `serviceName` equal to it** -
   `serviceName` is null for a layer that never loaded, so a missing title cannot pass for a resolved one (the #2720
   trap).
   - **Development: 98 of 98.**
   - **Production host: 93 of 98.** The five are Sustainable Transportation's own layers: the map is development-only,
     so production sends its link to the main map. They read the same TS/BikeMap layers as the main map's five, which
     passed there.
4. **Registry rows went with their titles**, in the same commit; the registry spec passes.

Four specs that found layers by the old title or asserted it were updated: the football lots are found by id, the
football micromobility and main-map bike specs now assert no title is declared.
