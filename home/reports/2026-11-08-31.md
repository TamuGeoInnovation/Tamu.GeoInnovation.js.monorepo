## The fix

When the hit test finds nothing with a popup, the click handler asks the layers at that point. This is the path the
trip planner already used. The new function is `queryPopupLayersAtClick`, in
`libs/maps/esri/src/lib/services/map/click-query.ts`, called from `MapService`'s handler.

What I settled:

- **Which layers:** only those with a `popupComponent` that can be queried, and only while their layer view is still
  being made or is `updating`. A layer view that is suspended (hidden, or out of scale) is skipped. Once every popup
  layer has drawn, an empty hit test is the truth.
- **Bare ground:** a click on bare ground on a drawn map sends **no request**. During the first seconds it asks only
  the layers still drawing.
- **Order:** layers are asked top-most first, as a hit test answers, and the results then go through
  `orderHitsForPopup` unchanged, so the highlight still goes behind. Queried features come before any hit without a
  popup, such as the basemap's vector tiles.
- **Points and lines** get an 8 px tolerance, from `view.resolution`. Polygons are found by intersection.
- **A failing layer's query** is left out, and the other layers' features still open.
- **Two quick clicks:** an answer that arrives after a later click's is dropped. Without that, a slow fallback could
  replace the newer popup.
- **The trip planner route** is unchanged: no emission and no fallback.
