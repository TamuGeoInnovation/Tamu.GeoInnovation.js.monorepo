## 2. The counts table

`admin-submissions.component.html` wraps the table in `<tamu-gisc-dashboard-scroll>`. **I kept `floating-table`:** it is
what gives the table every admin table's look, and inside the box its `width: 100%` fills the box on a wide screen (821
px at 1280, as the laptop measured). On a phone, the table's 377 px narrowest is wider than the box, so the box scrolls
sideways and the page doesn't. Dropping the class would only change how it looks on a wide screen, not whether it fits.

## 3. The test, red first

the page does not": the table sits inside `tamu-gisc-dashboard-scroll`, as a direct child of its `.dashboard-scroll`
element, the one with `overflow-x: auto`.
- **Red** on `92ec0bf33`: `closest('tamu-gisc-dashboard-scroll')` was `null`.

wider than itself (`scrollWidth` greater than `clientWidth`). The laptop's measurement at 360 and 390 will show it on the
