## What the prototype is

Three files; each change is marked `PROTOTYPE (#2459)` in the code.

1. **Hide the fill.** `makeBaseLayer` (`libs/maps/esri/src/lib/services/map/basemap-layers.ts`) takes a new `hiddenStyleLayers`
   list. Once the vector tile layer has loaded, it calls `setStyleLayerVisibility(id, 'none')` on each. The basemap names
   `Campus Structures/Currently Under Construction/1`, the `fill` layer whose `fill-pattern` is the published sprite (checked in
   `VTBase3`'s `root.json`). `.../0`, the black outline (`line`, `#000000`, 1.33), is left alone.
   - This is a style call; it never touches the sprite sheet.
   - I used `setStyleLayerVisibility` rather than the `setPaintProperties` call tested on dev today: it removes the fill
     outright, and it is the other candidate the note named. Either would do.
2. **Draw the buildings.** `makeBaseLayer` can now make a `FeatureLayer`, and `MapService.makeMap` loads that class.
   `AggiemapVectorBasemap` gets a second base layer:
   - `FCOR/TAMU_Basemap_20261008/MapServer/2` on `gis.tamu.edu`, `Type = 'CONSTRUCTION'` (8 buildings);
   - a simple renderer with a `CIMPolygonSymbol` of two `CIMHatchFill`s, at 0° and 90°, 12 pt apart, with 1.5 pt orange
     (`[230, 152, 0]`) strokes. That's the sprite's grid: 16 px apart and 2 px wide, the density the maintainer chose;
   - `listMode: 'hide'` and `popupEnabled: false`.

   Because it's part of the vector basemap, it shows only where that basemap does.
3. **No sprite.** `SPRITE_OVERRIDES_ENABLED = false` in `basemap-layers.ts`, so the definition's `spriteOverrides` is read but
   not applied. The definition is unchanged.

`BaseMapProperties.baseLayers` was a one-element tuple, `[LayerProperties]`; it is now an array, with the three feature-layer
properties added to `LayerProperties`. `nx build aggiemap-angular --configuration=development` passes.

## Do the two datasets agree?

On the 8 buildings the service lists, yes: wherever the service says `CONSTRUCTION`, the tiles draw the construction fill.
- **1266, 1524, 1526, 1539, 1819, 6891, 8130, 8711:** each loaded with `?bldg=` on both builds, counting the fill's colours in
  the map area.
- On the control, the fill shows pink (1524, 1526, 1819, 6891) or orange (1266, 1539, 8130, 8711), depending on whether the
  override took effect.
- On the prototype there is no pink at any of them, and the orange hatch is in the same places.

What this can't show: a building the tiles fill that the service does not list. The tiles cannot be queried for that. Such a
building would show the fill hidden and no hatch: it would draw as an outline only.

## What it costs

- **One query per map load**: `MapServer/2` with `Type = 'CONSTRUCTION'`, 8 polygons, on `gis.tamu.edu`. That is production's
  GIS server, under a basemap that is development-only, which is no new exposure: the basemap itself is on `gis.tamu.edu` too.
- **Two sources of truth.** The hatch now comes from the buildings service, and the outline from the tiles. They agree today on
  these 8, but a building whose `Type` changes before the tiles are re-cooked would be hatched with no outline, or outlined with
  no hatch. The sprite route had only one source.
- **A dated service.** `TAMU_Basemap_20261008` will be replaced like the tile caches. The URL belongs beside the basemap's and
  should change with it.
- **The view's projection.** The basemap is in EPSG:32139; the layer, in Web Mercator, is projected client-side. The projection
  engine is already loaded for the basemap.
