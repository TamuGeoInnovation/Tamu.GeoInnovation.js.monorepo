## What production actually had

(`sidebar-trip-planner.component.html`, `@if (dev | async)`), and production shows "The Aggie Map routing services are
currently undergoing maintenance and are unavailable" in its place. **The leak was the page by its address:
`/map/d/trip/options` opened Accessible Friendly Directions on production.** That is closed. The link gate is for when
directions go to production. The PR body says this first, so nobody reads the title and assumes the panel was visible.

  (#2801).

  production its address goes back to the trip planner.
- **Empty bar:** with both children hidden, `.options-container` has no height of its own, only horizontal padding.
  This cannot be seen on any host today: production hides the whole planner, and development shows both children.

## Not testable locally

The whole spec as production also fails `development-only services` on Nature Rx. A `--configuration=development`
build includes the layers a production build leaves out, so build-time gates cannot be tested by serving a development
build as production. It is unrelated to this change, and it is a limit of the local method worth knowing.
