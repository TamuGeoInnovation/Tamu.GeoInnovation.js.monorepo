## #2588: branch ready, `fix/2588-tile-tap-target` (fork, `52c7d8c60`)

**Rule 4: half of it was already done.** #3198 (PR 3199, merged 18 November) made a tap anywhere on a tile follow it, with
tests. The batch's red test ("a click on the icon or padding navigates") passes on development, so it could not be the red
test. What was still missing was the issue's keyboard half: no tile was focusable, so Tab skipped the menu entirely
(the built app: 25 presses, none on a tile), there was no link or button role, and no visible focus.

**The fix**, in `libs/ui-kits/ngx/navigation/mobile-tile` only; callers' markup unchanged:
- The tile host has `tabindex="0"` and `role="link"` when it has `tamuGiscTileLink`, or `role="button"` when it has a submenu
  (About, Account). Enter acts as a tap; Space does too for a submenu tile.
- A tile with neither stays inert, so GIS Day's Login tile (its own `tabindex`/keys on its title) keeps one tab stop.
- `:host(:focus-visible)` draws a 2px `$aggie-maroon` outline, offset 2px, like the accordion header.
- `tamu-gisc-tile-icon` is `aria-hidden="true"`: the built app showed each tile's name as "widgets Sessions" because the icon is
  a ligature word.
