**One finding is new:** dev does have the space counts, under another table name. That is a one-line ask of
Transportation, not a missing dataset. I recorded it in the pin's reason.

**What I did.** Layer 6 gets its own connection, `tsParkingLotsUrl`, and only that one is in
`READ_FROM_PRODUCTION_ON_DEVELOPMENT`. Every space-count reader already went through layer 6, so each now reads
`tsParkingLotsUrl`:
- Transportation Parking;
- the `all-parking` and `visitor-parking` search sources, and through them drive directions;
- the parking map's Parking Lots;
- the smoke suite's search-source expectations.

`tsMainUrl` is unpinned, so Visitor Parking and the rest of TS_Main read the development server.

**Why a second connection rather than per-layer pinning:** it uses the one mechanism the list already has, and this
is the only reason in the list that applies to one layer. Per-layer pinning would be a second way to pin, built for one case.

**Is per-layer pinning the right shape in general? Not yet.** Every other entry is about a whole service: no hosted
view, a token, or no layers. If a second layer-level reason turns up, it is worth its own issue. Until then, a
connection per differing layer is the smaller answer.

## 2. Construction: the pin stays, with its real reason

- **The two extra fields** on the hosted view are `SHAPE__Length` and `SHAPE__Area`. ArcGIS adds them itself, and no
  map reads them. The other eleven fields differ only in case, which the construction popup already handles (it reads
  them case-insensitively, for exactly this reason).
- **The fields are not the problem: the geometry is.** Dev's `TS/TSConstruction` MapServer answers queries with no
  geometry at all: no `geometryType`, no shapes, just attributes. That is the fault that retired production's copy, as
  `construction-map.definitions.ts` records. A map reading it would list 21 projects and draw nothing.
- So the pin stays, with that reason written in the list beside it. #2722's field-case answer would not change this.
  Dev needs the hosted view published (#1745 has the row), or its MapServer repaired.
