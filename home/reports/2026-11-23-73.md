## What changed in the port

- Meanwhile, development gave the session details email together sends (#3104), a chosen sender (#3100), a send record (#3167)
  and the shared in-memory `RecentSends`. The guard is ported onto that. A group's speakers are recorded in
  `session_details_sends` before the email goes, then checked; a repeat or a failed send removes them. So a together email is
  guarded across both API copies. The controller is development's, with no `sentBy` argument.
- **Found by the full check, fixed:** two together emails racing were both skipped. Each row had its own id, so the two claims
  were ordered differently for different speakers and each lost on one. Every row of a claim now has the claim's time and an
  id starting with the claim's (`<claim uuid>-<n>`), so one claim wins on every speaker. **Against a real SQL Server**, with
  made-up people: two copies racing a together email sent exactly one; both rows kept the claim's one time (TypeORM keeps a
  `created` it is given); a copy each to one of them afterwards was skipped as a repeat.
- The specs use the shared in-memory table (`_base/memory-table.ts`, from #3239). It learns `MoreThan` and sets `created`,
  and replaces the spec's own `sendsTable`. A new test covers a together group across two copies.
- `scrub.sql` keeps `session_details_sends` as `guid,sessionGuid,speakerGuid`.
- The release note names the new migration.
