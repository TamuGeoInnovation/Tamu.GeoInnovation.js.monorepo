## PR body: #1828
Title: fix(aggiemap-ngx-core): View as production stands above the notice panel while it shows

Closes #1828

## What this changes

On a development host, the **View as production** control and the grouped notice panel are both fixed at the bottom centre, and the control is drawn above the panel (z-index 1000 against 999). With two or more notices, it covered the panel's **1 of 2** arrows, so the later notices could only be reached by waiting for the timer. Production does not show the control.

**Option 2 is out: no corner is free on every map.** Read from a running build at 1440 wide and at phone width, on the main map, Football, Visitor Parking and Galveston:
- **Top left:** the zoom and compass controls (desktop); the phone's search bar spans the top.
- **Top right:** the sidebar's tabs (desktop).
- **Bottom left:** the help button (desktop).
- **Bottom right:** a map's own overlay zone on some maps (Football, desktop and phone); the locate button on the phone.
- The panel also has `left` and `right` variants at 2.5rem that a map may use.

**Option 3, in a smaller shape than proposed.**
- Whether the panel is showing is the panel's own state, not the notification service's: its ten-second countdown, the hover pause, and holding back behind a modal (#1246). A control reading the service would move at the wrong times.
- So **the panel publishes its height, as `--notification-grouped-height` on the page, while it shows**, and removes it when it hides, following a `ResizeObserver` as it pages between notices.
- The control's `bottom` is `calc(0.75rem + var(--notification-grouped-height, 0px))`, with the panel's own 250 ms easing, so it stands just above the panel and slides back down when it closes.
- **No dependency between the two libraries in either direction.** Anything else fixed to the bottom centre can stand clear of the panel the same way.
- **Production is unchanged:** nothing there reads the property, and the control is not drawn.

**Option 1 (lowering the z-index) was not taken:** it puts the control under the panel, unreachable while a notice shows, which the issue's *Done when* rules out.
