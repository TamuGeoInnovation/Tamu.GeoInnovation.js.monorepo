## The layer order, which took three tries

Found in the browser, not by the unit tests:

1. **Listed first, `essential`**: the construction drew **under** the campus tiles. RIB-A drew across the grid and cut our outline.
2. **Tiles pinned to the bottom with `layerIndex: 0`**: the construction was on top, but **the building labels vanished on both
   campuses**. The near-invisible click overlay now sat above the tiles, and hid their labels even with the construction turned off
   (shown by reordering the layers in a loaded page).
3. **The actual rule:** an event map adds its layers in the reverse of its **layer-id enum** (`references: FORT_WORTH_LAW_LAYERS`),
   one at a time, so the **first enum entry draws on top**; the order of the source list decides nothing. Its `essential` layers
   are made at start-up as each one loads. So `CONSTRUCTION` is the first enum entry and is not essential.
   - **Five loads of each campus, every one:** `property < basemap < construction`.
   - **The labels are back**, and the overlay sits under the tiles as before.

The test checks the real rule (the first enum id, not essential, no `layerIndex` on the tiles). **Each version was red against the
one before it.**
