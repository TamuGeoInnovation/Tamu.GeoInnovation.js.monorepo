## Findings

- **Draw order is load order.** `findLayerOrCreateFromSource` adds each layer when it finishes creating, so a map's array
  order is a strong hint but not a guarantee. `construction-map.definitions.ts` already documents that `layerIndex` is clamped
  and that "order of insertion is what determines the final z-position". A map that needs a line over polygons could add
  them in sequence. For the director; suggested priority Low unless the shuttle is seen under a lot again.
