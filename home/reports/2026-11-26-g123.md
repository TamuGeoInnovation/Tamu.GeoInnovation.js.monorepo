## What it does

**1. Branches.** After `git fetch --prune` of `origin` and `fork`, `branch_verdict` decides each local branch.

It **goes** when:
- its tip is in `origin/development`;
- every commit is on a remote and its fork branch is gone;
- or its pull request merged and nothing was committed after the merge.

**How I read the issue's rule.** #3707 says "fork branch gone, or tip in development" goes, and "commits on no
remote" stays. Those collide for every squash-merged branch: its own commits are on no remote, but its fork branch is
gone. On home that was 425 of 452 branches. So I made the deciding question "did its pull request merge, with nothing
committed after?". That is the rule g122 applied by hand.

It **stays and is listed** "only on this machine" when anything else has commits on no remote. It stays as "open work"
when its fork branch is still there.

It **never** removes `development`, `master`, any name containing `mailbox`, a local copy of one (`mb-*`), or a branch
held by a worktree that is kept.

**2. Your three additions:**
- **Worktree before branch:** `prune_steps` does a branch's worktree first, then the branch. If the worktree removal
  fails, the branch is left.
- **Leftovers count as clean:** a worktree whose only untracked files are `.pr-body.md`, `check.out`,
  `check-tamu-js-*.log` or `.b<n>/`.
  - The `.b<n>/` folders on home were listener batch scratch, from issues that all shipped (g122's verdicts). So they
    are in.
  - `only_leftovers` replaces `only_a_drafted_pr_body`. Screenshots and anything else still keep a worktree.
- **Old checkout:** `branch_in_development` always asks `refs/remotes/origin/development` after the fetch, never
  `HEAD`. A test builds a real repository whose checkout is older than a branch merged into `origin/development`.
  - **The sweep also never removes the worktree it is run in**, or that worktree's branch. That is g122's
    `wt-preprcheck` problem, fixed by `without_path`, which ignores case and slash direction.

**3. Every check.** `check-in-volume.sh` now runs `tidy.sh --branches` whenever it is not sweeping for disk, and shows
only the lines saying what it removed. `--branches`:
- removes finished branches and their worktrees;
- **touches no volume**, so volumes stay behind the watermark;
- **leaves detached worktrees to the free-space sweep.** They belong to no branch, and several are tools other sessions
  keep and reuse here: `wt-preprcheck`, and other listeners' own checkouts such as `wt-codex-home`. Deleting them on
  every check would break those sessions.
