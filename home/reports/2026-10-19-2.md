## What changed
- `<arcgis-map>` makes the 2D view and `<arcgis-scene>` the 3D one (drawn beside it the first time 3D is asked for; the
  hidden one stays on the page). Each map still opens where it did: its view settings are set before the element loads.
- `EsriMapService` no longer makes views (`makeMap`, `useView`); it still hands out a real MapView/SceneView, so the layer
  list, legend, popups, search, selection, directions and the map probe are unchanged. 2D/3D switching shows the other
  element instead of swapping containers.
- Controls are in the elements' slots: zoom from the map component (a `zoom` input, off on phones), `<arcgis-track>`, our
  compass and the Nearmap slider in the page templates; in 3D, Esri's zoom, pan/rotate and compass components.
- **No `view.ui` anywhere in the repository.** Deleted: `map-controls.ts`, the `ui`/`mode` settings, the unused 3D
  start-up path. Attribution is hidden by `attributionVisible: false` (the view now sits in the element's shadow root).
- **Outside AggieMap:** Signage, Geoservices and GIS Day's competition maps share the map component, so they moved too
  (they keep their zoom buttons). Only through the shared component; nothing else in those apps changed.
