## The service first, as the batch asked

- Layer 1 of `FCOR/MapInfo_20190529` (*Family Friendly Restroom Feature Layer*) names the field **`BldgNum`**, filled on
  **116 of 116** restrooms. There is no `Bldg_Number`. So it isn't a missing field, and the stop rule didn't apply.
- **But it was not a one-word fix either, which makes it the third this week.** The component the issue names,
  `RestroomPopupComponent`, **has been unused since #418 (August 2025)**. The restroom layer opens the generic markdown popup,
  filled from a `popupData` template in its layer source: *Building: {Name}*, *Restroom Location(s): {Notes}*. That is what
  production shows today. It never shows a building number, and a template can't leave out an empty row. Renaming the field in
  the unused template alone would have changed nothing a visitor sees, as with #657.
- **And the field is not right everywhere.** Compared with the campus buildings layer (`TAMU_Basemap_20261008/1`, `Number`):
  104 restrooms match a building of the same name, and 9 more a building under its newer name. **Wrong:**
  - **the Student Services Building is 0416 in the service**, but no building 0416 exists; SSB is 1546;
  - 8701 (Rowlett) names the 4-H headquarters at RELLIS;
  - 0468.1 (Evans) is not a building number, though it may mean the annex.

  **Filed as #2529** (Bug, `bug`, assigned, Texas A&M Aggiemap, Todo, no priority). It's a data fault for Facilities Analytics
  & Mapping; nothing changes here.

## What changed

1. **The restroom layer opens `RestroomPopupComponent`**, and its `popupData` template is deleted. One way to draw this popup
   instead of two, and the one that can hide a row.
2. **The popup:**
   - is headed *Unisex Restroom* (every record's `Type`);
   - shows the building name, then **Building `BldgNum`**, only when there is one;
   - shows **Restroom location(s)** from `Notes` as HTML, as the lactation popup does, because 9 records use `<br>` for one
     floor per line. The row is left out when there are no notes.
3. **No Directions To Here** on this popup now, as on the lactation popup. The markdown popup had one; directions are hidden
   on production anyway (#659).
