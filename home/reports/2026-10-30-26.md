## Could Men's Basketball use the helper? No, and why

It builds its three sources by hand for a reason the helper cannot express without growing into a general source
builder:
- **its popups differ.** `MarkdownPopupComponent` titles a popup from `attributes.name`, and a source's `popupData`
  overwrites the attributes before it opens. The helper always maps `name`/`description`. Men's **Basketball Icons
  (`/0`) has no popup at all**, and **Basketball Closures (`/1`) titles from `type` and describes from `event`**. Only its
  parking (`/2`) matches;
- **its production service is a different one**: `MBskt_view` on the production GIS server (#1749), not the
  republished development schema the helper is written for.

So folding it in would need "no popup" and "popup fields" options, which is a bigger helper for one caller. Left as it is.
