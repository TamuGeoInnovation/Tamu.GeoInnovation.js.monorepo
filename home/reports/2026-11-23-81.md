## What this changes

<!-- What a reader needs to know. Why, not only what. -->

The Presentation and Poster competition pages typed 2026's dates into their text: the heading's day, the submission deadline and its time, and, on the Posters page, the poster session's day in the finalists' instructions. Next year they would be wrong unless someone remembered to change them.

This is the half of #3019 that needs no new decision:

- **The heading's day** is the competition sessions' own: the undergraduate and graduate sessions on Presentation, the poster session on Posters. They are found as the pages already find them, by `competitionSession`. With both presentation sessions on one day, it's that day; on two days, both. With none found, there is no heading line.
- **Due** is the season's submission deadline (`presentationSubmissionsCloseAt`, `posterSubmissionsCloseAt`, #2934), the one the site enforces. It's in Central time and replaces the typed date and time lines. With none set, it reads "to be announced".
- **The poster finalists' line** gives the poster session's day, or no day.
- **`sessionOfKind` and `competitionDays`** are exported from the competition session component, which now uses `sessionOfKind` itself, so the pages and the component find sessions one way.

**Not changed, waiting for a decision:**
- the day finalists are notified, which has no season setting yet;
- choosing each competition's sessions in Admin, instead of by the `COMPETITION:`/`AWARDS:` naming convention;
- the Monuments and VGI pages.
