## Cause (not PR 1950)
The service's own renderer for `TS_Events/BusinessParking/MapServer/1` is unique-value on `GIS.TS.Lot_Use.UB_Lot` **and**
`GIS.TS.ParkingLots.LotType`, with no default symbol. **The service does not publish `GIS.TS.ParkingLots.LotType`**: it is
not in the layer's 49 fields, and a query naming it as an output field answers `400 Failed to execute query` (a `where` can
still use it, which is why every count the work machine took was right). So every lot arrives without `LotType`, the renderer
has no symbol for it, and nothing is drawn. In headless Chromium on production the two layer views **held 73 and 6 lots** and
the map showed none; the legend entries were empty.

Your hypothesis (two layers sharing `/1`) is discarded: a fresh layer on `/1` with a plain renderer draws; the shared URL is
not the problem. The legend errors in the console are a second, smaller fault: our legend compares the integer `UB_Lot` as a
string (`UB_Lot = '1'`), which this server rejects. Filed as **#1988** (Bug, no priority; suggested Medium).

**Note on the browser pane:** measuring this in the desktop app's browser pane gave "updating, 0 drawn" for every layer,
including the spaces that do draw: a hidden pane runs as a background page and does not draw. Headless Chromium in the
Playwright container is the reliable instrument.

## Fix
Each lot layer's definition expression already selects one of the renderer's two groups, so each now draws with **one fill in
the colour the service's legend gives that group**: maroon `[90,0,0]` (UB and UB+), pink `[232,190,255]` (UB+ only). The
override carries its reason (as `docs/map-layers.md` asks) and goes when the service publishes `LotType`. Applied by hand to
production's own layers in headless Chromium first: every lot drew, and the legend filled in.
