## The design

**One nullable column, `seasons.competitionTypes`** (`nvarchar(255)`): the research competitions the season runs,
comma-separated. **Empty runs every one**, so every existing season, the UCGIS site and a newly cloned season behave as
now until an admin chooses. One definition, `libs/gisday/common/src/lib/season-competitions.ts`, sits beside the deadline
rules and reads the same competition table. VGI is left to the site's `competitions` feature, as the issue says.

- **Admin:** a **Competitions** section above the deadlines on Research Competitions, with a box per competition, saved
  through `PATCH seasons/:guid/competition-types` (`update:competitions`, as the deadlines are). At least one must be
  ticked; all four are stored as empty. The deadlines then list only the competitions running, and a competition turned
  off keeps its deadlines for when it is turned back on.
- **Admin filters:** unchanged. The submissions list's type filter already offers only the types the season's
  submissions have (#2817), so a turned-off competition's submissions stay listed, as the issue requires.
- **The form** offers only the competitions running, plus a saved submission's own type. When Presentation isn't running,
  a new submission starts on the first competition that is.
- **The Competitions page** shows its Poster and Presentation blocks only in a season that runs them.
- **The API** refuses a new submission to, or a move into, a competition the season doesn't run, with 400. An existing
  submission may still be edited.
