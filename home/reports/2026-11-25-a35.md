## Where the builder is, and why

`satelliteCampus()` is in `libs/ts/events/ngx/src/lib/definitions/campus/satellite-campus.ts`, beside `campus-overlay.definitions.ts`:
- only satellite campuses use it;
- the overlay it builds on is already there;
- and, as you narrowed it, `common.definitions.ts` is a36's (#2599).

## What it takes

**The campus:** id, name, introduction, centre and zoom, the layer-id enum, and the basemap.

**The building layer:** its URL, and whether it is called "Property" or "Building". Two campuses call the layer "Buildings" and its
search "Building", which is now one switch rather than two strings.

**The field triple:** required, with no default.

**Only where a campus has them:**
- a popup title list (Galveston's four fields);
- no address (McAllen);
- its own window title or Discover text (DC, Galveston, McAllen);
- a construction layer (Fort Worth Law School, Houston Space Institute);
- a postcode filter (HSC College of Dentistry).

**Each campus file keeps** its enum and the comments that are about it: centre, zoom, layer index, branding TODOs. The generic comments
are written once, in the builder.
