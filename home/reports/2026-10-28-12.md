## What changed

1. **`aggiemapBasemap()` returns the vector basemap everywhere.**
   - It no longer takes `isTesting`. `isTesting ? vector : vector` was the alternative, and a parameter that does nothing is
     worse than none.
   - Its callers are the main map, the event map and its spec. They are updated, and every map still resolves the campus
     basemap there, so the next version can be gated in one place.
   - Its comment is rewritten to say why it was production's safe default until now, and what changed.
2. **Production's gallery is `[vector, raster]`**, option A, with the director's reason in the comment.
   - The raster entry **has its own id on production, `aggie_basemap_raster`**, titled *Aggieland raster*. The plain
     `AggiemapRasterBasemap` has `aggie_basemap`, the same id as the vector basemap, and the gallery marks and restores
     choices by id. So it is the plain raster basemap under its own id, not the dated development variant.
   - `basemapForSavedId` needs no change: a visitor who saved `aggie_basemap` now gets the vector basemap, and one who picks the
     raster entry restores it by its own id.
   - **The vector basemap's title is now plain *Aggieland***, as its comment asked once it became production's.
3. **`DEVELOPMENT_ONLY_SERVICES` no longer lists `Hosted/VTBase3/VectorTileServer`**; `Hosted/VTBase/VectorTileServer`
   (September) stays, and its comment is updated.
4. **The view limit stays at 1:282, with a reason, and the check now follows what production offers:**
   - **I looked first.** Below 1:282 the vector basemap stays crisp, so it isn't mush, but it draws nothing new: the same paths and
     grass, larger, and the trees shrink to dots and are gone by 1:70.5. The pictures are on the branch:
     `vector-plaza-scale282.png`, `-scale141.png` and `-scale71.png` (Academic Plaza).
   - **Raising it would bring back #1577 for the raster basemap.** One limit applies to every view, and production still offers
     the raster basemap, whose cache stops at 1:282 (some maps also open on it). A view allowed to 1:70.5 goes white there.
   - **`services.spec.ts`'s view-limit test** now asks every campus basemap production offers (vector and raster) for its
     `maxScale`, and requires the limit to match the shallowest. Run against the real services:
     - with the limit at 282: **passes**;
     - with it set to 70 for one run, then put back: **fails**, saying `AGGIEMAP_BASEMAP_MAX_SCALE is 70, but the shallowest
       campus basemap production offers, .../TAMU_Basemap_20261008/MapServer, caches down to 282.124294 (VTBase3: 70.53; ...)`.
   - The test also had to stop using `basemapRoots` to find the raster service. That list keeps one name per service, and the
     vector basemap now names the raster basemap's service too, for its construction layer, so the raster name was lost. The
     first run failed on exactly that.
5. **One caller the batch didn't name.** The basemap gallery's 3D stand-in (the raster basemap a scene shows in place of the
   State Plane vector one) fell back to `aggiemapBasemap(false)`, meaning "production's raster". That would now have been the
   vector basemap again. It falls back to `aggiemapRasterBasemap(...)` by name.
