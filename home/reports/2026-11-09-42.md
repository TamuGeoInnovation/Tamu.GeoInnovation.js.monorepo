## The change

- **The tile** is `isDev`.
- **The page** is gated on its route. `DevelopmentOnlyMapGuard` also reads `developmentOnly` in a route's `data`, and
  sends a page that is not a map back to the page above it (`/all-maps`). It is now exported from `ts-events`. This
  extends the existing guard instead of adding a second one.
- **The smoke suite:** `Transportation` joins `DEVELOPMENT_ONLY_ALL_MAPS_TILES`, and `DEVELOPMENT_ONLY_ALL_MAPS_PAGES`
  is new.
- **`development-only-gates.spec.ts` compares tiles and pages too, both ways.** `VISIT_MAPS_TILES` is exported for it,
  and the pages are read from `discoverRoutes`.

**The guard question in the batch.** The section route is enough. Both maps' direct addresses already go to `/map` on a
production build, through their own `developmentOnly`: the existing check, 6 of 6, covers
`/transportation/micromobility` and `/transportation/sustainable-transportation`, and their old `/parking/`
addresses.
