## Part 2: #3467, an event notice still shows on the evening of its event. Branch ready: `fix/3467-event-notice-evening` (`2315b1822`)

**The fix.** `raiseActive()` now uses the shared `nextEventDate` and `parseEventDate`, with no new date arithmetic:
- an event's notice is raised while its next date's whole local day has not ended, and that date is within a week;
- the season year is `getFullYear` of the earliest parsed date.

That is 11 lines in and 25 out.

**Red first, with a fixed clock and a real zone.** This took one more step than expected:
- `event-notifications.evening.spec.ts` freezes the clock at 8 PM Central on a made-up 2 October event, and expects the notice. At 8 PM the
  next day it expects none. Its first test asserts that the zone is Central, so it cannot pass by accident in UTC.
- Setting `process.env.TZ` inside the spec does **not** work. Vitest gives each spec its own copy of `process.env`, so the zone assertion
  failed and the run stayed in UTC, where this bug cannot appear at all.
- So `libs/aggiemap/ngx/core/vitest.config.mts` sets `TZ = 'America/Chicago'` before the workers start, with a comment saying why. The
  zone is set in the test configuration, never in the shell.
- With that, against the unfixed service, in a UTC container: the zone test passed, "raises the notice at 8 PM Central on the day of the
  event" **failed** (`expected [] to have a length of 1`), and the day-after test passed.
- With the fix, all 99 of the project's tests pass, in a UTC container and in a Central one. The other 7 notification specs pass in Central
  too.

**Check:** `Exit 0 (fix/3467-event-notice-evening, aggiemap-ngx-core,aggiemap-angular)`.
