## What it does

- **Table `season_winners`:** season, competition, place (1 to 3), submission, and `accountGuid` for a trail
  winner. Unique per season, competition and place. A row goes when its season or its submission is deleted.
  Migration `1795444106838-AddSeasonWinners` only adds the table.
- **API:** `GET /season-winners` is public. It returns the active season's winners only from the day after its last
  day (Central time), and only place, competition, names and title. `GET` and `PUT /season-winners/season/:guid`
  need `read:competitions` and `update:competitions`, which already exist. A save replaces the judged places in one
  transaction, and refuses an entry that isn't that competition's accepted entry that season.
- **Admin page:** Research Competitions, then Winners. Three boxes per competition, choosing from the accepted entries
  by title and names. Nothing is typed; names and titles are read from the submission when shown.
- **Homepage:** a "<year> Competition Winners" section in plain text, on gisday.tamu.edu only (new `winners` site
  feature, off on txgisday.org and UCGIS).
- **Trail challenge:** the table can hold its winners by account, and the Winners page has a section saying they
  aren't picked yet. **No picker built**: it needs the VGI submitters as choices and their names from Auth0, which
  doesn't fall out of the research one. The homepage leaves out a trail place until it can be named.
