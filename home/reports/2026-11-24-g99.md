## What this changes

<!-- What a reader needs to know. Why, not only what. -->

Ten data-api providers each wrote their own "records for the active season" and "copy records into another season", and a fix reached one copy at a time: the season-not-found guard, the nothing-found guard, the `await` that lets a failed save be caught. The catch block that passes an `HttpException` through had been pasted 62 times in 17 files (#3324).

**What changed**
- **`_base/rethrow-http.ts`**: `rethrowHttp(err, fallback, log?)` passes an `HttpException` through and throws `fallback` for anything else, after `log` if given. It replaces all 62 pasted blocks; each keeps its own fallback and logging.
- **`_base/season-scoped-provider.ts`**: `SeasonScopedProvider<T>`, extended by all ten providers (class, event, event-broadcast, event-location, organization, place, speaker, sponsor, tag, university). Each says how it reads a season's records (`forSeason`) and what its records are called.
  - **`forActiveSeason()`**: every provider's active-season method uses it. The `throw 422 'No active season found.'` after `findOneActive()` is gone: `findOneActive` throws 404 itself and never resolves empty, so that line could not run.
  - **`copyIntoSeason(seasonGuid, guids)`**: tag, university, event-broadcast, event-location and class copy through it. Each now refuses a missing season (422), refuses when none of the records is found (422), awaits its save, and answers a failed save with 422 and a log line, as university already did. A copy is the record without its guid, times or season; class's copy also leaves out its students, and a location's its place and sessions, as before.
- **Event, organization, place, speaker and sponsor keep their own copy methods.** They clone nested records (logos, links, speakers), so they do not fit the plain copy. Moving them is follow-up work.

**What a client sees differently**
- **Copying broadcasts** now refuses a missing season or no matching broadcasts (422). It used to write broadcasts into no season, or answer an empty list.
- **A failed save while copying** answers 422 everywhere. Class and location copies answered 500, or a raw driver error, because their saves were not awaited.
- **A failed lookup while copying broadcasts** is no longer reported as 422 ("your request was wrong"). Nest answers it 500.
- **Refusal messages** read the same across the five: `Season not found.` and `Could not find <records> to copy.`
