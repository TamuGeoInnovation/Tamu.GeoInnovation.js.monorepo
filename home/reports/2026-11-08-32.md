## The gate (in 2725, and used by both)

Nothing in the repository could keep a **whole map** off production. `discover.visible: false` hides a
map from production's lists but leaves its link working, and `developmentSources` swaps layers by GIS
server rather than by `isTesting`. So 2725 adds one field and one guard:

- **`developmentOnly: '<issue>: <why>'`** on a map's definition.
- **`DevelopmentOnlyMapGuard`** runs first on every event route. Where `TestingService.isTesting` is
  false, it sends the link to the main map before anything loads, so production never requests the
  service.
- **Discover** treats such a map as `visible: false`: listed on dev, nowhere on production.
- **`DEVELOPMENT_ONLY_SERVICES`** lists each service with its page (`/parking/micromobility`,
  `/parking/ncaa-volleyball`).
- **A unit spec** fails any `developmentOnly` map whose service is missing from that list. That is what
  makes the gate real: it cannot be marked development-only without the smoke suite also guarding it.

To move a map to production: delete its `developmentOnly` and its smoke entry together. For #2713, also
point `ncaaVolleyballUrl` at the production service once Transportation publishes it.
