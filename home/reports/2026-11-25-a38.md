## The finding: #387 does not reproduce in Chromium

**#3656's check passes on dev, so it is an ordinary check, not an expected failure.** I had expected it to fail: the a37 sweep read
#387 as "the list cannot scroll", from the stylesheet (`height: 100vh`, no `overflow-y`). Measured on dev at 375 by 380:
- The panel itself does not scroll: `overflow-y: visible`, `scrollTop` 0.
- **The page does.** `document.scrollingElement.scrollTop` reached 95 against a `scrollHeight` of 475, which brings the last layer into
  view.
- The sidebar then still closes when tapped beside it.

So in Chromium **both halves of #387 work today**: the list's end can be reached, and the sidebar dismisses. That is not proof for a
phone's browser. #387 was reported on one, and iOS Safari handles a page scroll under a fixed backdrop differently. Hence **Q-a38-1**.

Two things looked like #387 and were not:
- A tap beside the panel at mid-height hit a **notification's close button**, not the backdrop. At 380 px, "Map Available" and the
  development build's "View as production" cover the middle and bottom of the screen. The checks now tap and scroll near the top (y 40
  and 100).
- A tap **within the first second of load**, while the map is still starting, does not reach the sidebar, though the backdrop is
  already under it. The same tap a few seconds later closes it, at both 380 and 812 px. The checks wait for `waitForMapReady` first,
  as a person would. This is a start-up race rather than a fault anyone has reported; I mention it only so nobody files it as #387.
