## What that does and does not say
- **The issue itself says the fault is a race:** the reporter's own retry on the same build drew. So 60 passes do not prove it gone, but they make it rare at most on today's build and services.
- **Since 11 November, `setStops`'s door lookup** was made unable to stop the trip (#2851, the `refineStops` comment), and the search list now closes after a choice (#2844). Either may have removed the race. I can't tell which without the original failure.
- **One candidate I found reading the code, not shown to happen:** the constructor's settings subscription calls `clearRoute()` and then `executeTripTask()`. `executeTripTask` returns early when results already exist for the same travel modes. So a travel-option change after a route has drawn would clear the line and keep the panel, which is #2846's exact symptom. Every caller of `updateTravelOptions` I found is a visitor action (the mode picker, the options), not something automatic. So it does not explain a trip where the visitor only chose two ends. **Worth its own look; not claimed as #2846's cause.**
