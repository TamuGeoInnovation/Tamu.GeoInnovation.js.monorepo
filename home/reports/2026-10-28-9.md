## Task 2: the change

- **`makeBaseLayer`** (`libs/maps/esri/src/lib/services/map/basemap-layers.ts`):
  - `hiddenStyleLayers` on a `VectorTileLayer`: once the style loads, `setStyleLayerVisibility(id, 'none')` for each. It is a
    style call that never touches the sprite sheet, and the comment says why.
  - `FeatureLayer` is a base layer type, and a required member of `BaseLayerConstructors`.
  - `MapService.makeMap` loads `FeatureLayer` with the other classes.
- **The sprite override is deleted, not disabled**: `vector-tile-sprites.ts`, its spec (it tested only that module), its
  export from `libs/maps/esri/src/index.ts`, `spriteOverrides` on `LayerProperties`, the definition's `spriteOverrides` entry
  and its call site. No switch is left behind.
- **`AggiemapVectorBasemap`**:
  - the tiles hide `Campus Structures/Currently Under Construction/1`. The comment says why `/1` (the rasterized pink
    `fill-pattern`) is hidden and `/0` (the black outline, a plain line) is not;
  - a second base layer, `${TAMU_CAMPUS_BASEMAP_URL}/2` with `Type = 'CONSTRUCTION'`, draws the CIM grid hatch. The URL is
    now the shared constant, so it moves with the dated service;
  - the definition's doc comment is rewritten to describe this.
- **`BaseMapProperties.baseLayers` is now `Array<LayerProperties>`, not the one-element tuple `[LayerProperties]`.**
  `LayerProperties` gains `hiddenStyleLayers`, plus `definitionExpression`, `renderer` and `popupEnabled`, typed from
  `esri.FeatureLayerProperties`. A reviewer should look at this interface change. Every caller typechecks: the
  `aggiemap-angular` build passes. `makeBaseLayer` is called only by `MapService`.
