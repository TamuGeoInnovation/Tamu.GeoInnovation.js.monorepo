### What it was not, measured

- **Not the transparent symbol.** Once the buildings layer view had finished updating, headless Chromium hit every
  building in the view at alpha 0 (280 of 280 points), and so did headed Chromium (245 of 245). The director's 13 of 20
  was taken while the layer was still updating, and is withdrawn on #2707.
- **Not latency, and not "one click behind".** Real clicks name the building after 2 to 3 s in headless Chromium,
  because WebGL is software there and a hit test alone takes 0.7 to 1.4 s. The "previous building's popup" my scripts
  reported came from the hidden popup still in the page. On screen, a click on a dead building closes the popup, and
  the sidebar goes back to the map's description (the "before" picture). The spec now reads only a visible popup.
- **Not routing.** The trip planner asks the layer with `queryFeatures` and never hit-tests, so nothing about drawing
  affects it. Posted on #2707.
- **Clicking early is a lesser effect.** Clicking from the first second for 70 s on `/map/d` named 15 of 17. One miss
  came before the buildings layer existed, the other while it was still drawing.

## What the branch does

- **One connection, `buildingsUrl`**, pointing at `Hosted/VTBase3/FeatureServer/0`, "Campus Structures". The constant is
  `TAMU_CAMPUS_STRUCTURES_URL`, beside the campus basemap's.
- **Everything that read `basemapUrl/1` uses it:**
  - the Buildings layer;
  - the three building search sources;
  - the Dining kiosk's buildings;
  - the UES app's buildings and building search.
- **Every reader of building fields reads lower case:** the building popup, the search templates and the three
  viewfinders. The trip planner reads a stop's fields whatever their case, through a new `attributeOf`, because its
  stops come from services that spell them differently.
- **`number IS NOT NULL`** keeps 1,003 of 1,984 rows, with the same building numbers as the old layer's 1,003. The
  director's padding correction holds: `?bldg=` matches on `number`, which is unpadded on both services. I corrected
  #2722's claim on #2722.
- **A near-invisible fill**, `buildingsClickLayerNative`, shared by the main map and the kiosk. Beside it is why: the
  layer exists to be clicked; the service publishes opaque teal (`[100, 200, 200, 255]`), which would paint every
  building again; and #1526 removed it once. The after picture shows no teal.
- **`building-clicks.spec.ts`:** buildings come from the layer's own geometry, and eight are clicked on each of three
  maps. The visible popup must name the building clicked, and any failed buildings query fails the run on its own. Time
  is only recorded.
- **Unit specs:** the Buildings layer's source, filter and fill; the deep-link fields; `buildingsUrl` staying unmarked;
  `attributeOf`.
- **Release note** `docs/releases/unreleased/2707-buildings-from-vector-service.md`, with the forced "before" labelled as
  forced.
