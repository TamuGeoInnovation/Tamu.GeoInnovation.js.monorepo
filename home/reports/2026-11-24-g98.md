## What this changes

<!-- What a reader needs to know. Why, not only what. -->

US Central was written out as `'America/Chicago'` in about ten files, and a calendar day was worked out three times, with two different daylight-saving calculations. Only one caller, `inCentralTime`, replaced the narrow no-break space newer ICU puts before AM and PM (#3323).

**What changed**
- **`libs/gisday/common/src/lib/central-time.ts`**, exported from the library:
  - `GISDAY_TIME_ZONE`;
  - `formatCentral(date, options)`: any `Intl.DateTimeFormat` options, in Central time, with a plain space before AM and PM for every caller;
  - `centralDay(date)`: the Central calendar day, as YYYY-MM-DD;
  - `centralInstant(day, time)`: the instant of a Central wall-clock time on a day, by one daylight-saving calculation, the one `gisday-common.ts` already had, which handles any time of day.
- **Every caller uses them:**
  - `sentAt`, the profile email's link expiry, `sessionDay`, `inCentralTime`;
  - `checkInOpensAt`, `sessionTime`, `dayHasPassed`;
  - the accounts dashboard's `centralDay` and `centralMidnight`, which keep their names for their callers;
  - the sessions-using list, the site copy's times, a session's check-ins;
  - the event dashboard's CSV name.
- **The chart library is not changed:** `dayIn` and the functions built on it in `libs/ui-kits/ngx/charts` already take the zone as an argument, defaulting to Central, so AggieMap is untouched and GIS Day passes `GISDAY_TIME_ZONE`.

**The one change in what is printed:** the narrow no-break space. `sentAt`, the sessions-using times, `checkInOpensAt`, the site copy's times and a session's check-ins printed it before AM and PM, and now print a plain space, as `inCentralTime` already did. Nothing else changes, either side of the November and March changes.
