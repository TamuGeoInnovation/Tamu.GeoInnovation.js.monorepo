## What this changes

**A nightly smoke run that does not finish now says so, as loudly as one that fails.** From 3 to 27 November every scheduled run hit the job's `timeout-minutes: 120` and ended `cancelled`. The report upload and the health-issue steps ran on `failure()` only, so those nights produced no report and no health issue: nothing checked to the end, and nothing said so.

- **Playwright stops the suite itself at 105 minutes** (`--global-timeout=6300000`), inside the job's 120. Running out of time is then an ordinary failure: Playwright writes its report with the unreached tests listed as not run, the report artifact uploads, and the health issue opens or updates.
- **The upload and health-issue steps also run on `cancelled()`.** A job killed by its limit or by hand checked nothing to the end, which must be at least as loud as a failure.
- **The health issue's comment says which it was:** did not finish (cancelled, or out of time) or failing. The test output is kept (`tee smoke-output.txt`) so the step can tell.

**The cap is deliberately unchanged.** Whether two hours is too short is answered by measurement, and the measurement is what this change starts producing: no recent night has per-test timings, because cancelled runs uploaded nothing and the last finished run's artifacts have expired. On 27 November production kept reporting results until 13 minutes before the cut: the `chromium` project for about 89 minutes, then `heavy` from 12:52. So it is too short, not hanging, but by how much is for the next report to say. The suite is now 1,542 tests on 4 workers.
