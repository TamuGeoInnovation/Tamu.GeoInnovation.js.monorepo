**The fix, in `connections.ts`:**
- `tsgisHost` follows `gisHost`. A development build reads `gis.dev.tamu.edu`; production reads `arc.ts.tamu.edu` (`PRODUCTION_TS_HOST`).
- `TS_Events/<X>` on production becomes `TS/<X>` on development. The development server has no `TS_Events` folder, and its `TS` services have the same names, layers and fields. It is one rule, `tsEvents`, in one place.
- `athleticsHost` is retired into the same rule, and so are the four same-path hosted views, Muster and the five geometry-only services.
- The `TEMP` comment in `getDefaultGisHosts()` is gone, and that function now flips `tsgisHost` too.
- Construction (tailgating, the construction map, UES) now reads `Connections.constructionUrl` instead of building its own URL.
