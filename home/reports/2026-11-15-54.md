## What it does

All three places, the Speakers list (**Email edit links**), an event (**Email speakers their edit links**) and Research
Competitions (**Send profile email**), open **the same window the acceptance emails use**: the subject and the text,
both editable, today's as the default, with a preview for the first person. Cancel sends nothing.

- **One window:** `review-email-modal` moved unchanged to `ngx-common` (its own commit, `refactor`), because the event
  form is in `ngx-common` and can't import from `ngx-core`. It gained an editable subject and its own placeholder list.
  One helper, `sendProfileEmail`, beside it, is what all three callers use.
- **One path:** the existing `sendLinks`/`sendLinksForSubmissions` take the approved subject and text. With neither, the
  email is as always (a speaker asking for their own link still gets that). The submission-to-speaker matching became
  one `speakersOfSubmissions`, used by the send and its preview. New `send/preview` and
  `send-for-submissions/preview` keep their sends' permissions.
- **The link:** each speaker's is made and filled in only when their email is sent. The preview shows "(their own edit
  link, added when it is sent)". A text without `{edit link}`, or an empty subject, is refused (400) before any link
  is made.
- **The text:** `libs/gisday/common/src/lib/profile-email.ts`, today's subject and text with {first name}, {site},
  {site address}, {edit link} and {link expires}, filled by the review emails' own `fillReviewEmail`. One wording
  change: no first name now reads "Howdy there," (as the review emails do), not "Howdy,".
- Research Competitions' yes/no question became the window's note ("For the 3 people of 2 submissions...").
