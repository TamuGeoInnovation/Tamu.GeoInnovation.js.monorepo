## What this changes

`BusService` still carried the methods that called Transportation Services' retired API at
`nodes.geoservices.tamu.edu`, and its own comment said the API no longer exists. Dead code that calls a dead service
reads as a working integration: the next bus feature to call one of these gets a 502 at run time, with nothing to say
why. This deletes them: 774 lines out, 3 in.

**Deleted by reachability, not by the issue's list.** The issue names three methods, but `busesForRoute` had a caller,
`toggleBusLocations`, which had none. The set here is the transitive closure of what nothing live reaches.

- **Live, and untouched:**
  - the constructor (`init` and `busMapLayer`);
  - `getRoutes`, `toggleMapRoute`, `getRouteStops` and `removeAllFromMap`;
  - `busLayer` and `busLayerGraphics`;
  - `BUS_ROUTES_URL` and `serviceUrl`.

  These are what the bus map and `bus-timetable` use.
- **Methods removed:** `waypointsForRoute`, `stopsForRoute`, `timetableForRoute`, `timeTableForRouteOnDate`,
  `getStopAtPoint`, `generateTimetable`, `annotateBusGraphic`, `getTripDepartureArrivalStops`, `busesForRoute` and
  `toggleBusLocations`.
  - The roots: `waypointsForRoute` and `timetableForRoute` had no caller at all; `annotateBusGraphic` and
    `busesForRoute` had only commented-out ones; and `toggleBusLocations` had none.
  - The other five were reachable only from those.
- **Fields removed:**
  - `base_url`;

    `TimetableEntry` and `TimetableWithLinger`;

    files.
- **The commented-out call sites go too:**
  - `trip-planner.service`'s `annotateBusGraphic` case;
  - `bus-switch`'s `busesForRoute` block, with its `BusService` injection and the two fields (`passengerLoad`,
    `busCount`) that only that block set and nothing read.
