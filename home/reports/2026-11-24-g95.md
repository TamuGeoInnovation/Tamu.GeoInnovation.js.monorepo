## What this changes

<!-- What a reader needs to know. Why, not only what. -->

Names were sorted in reader's order in about ten places, each with its own options (`numeric` on or off, `'en'` or the browser's locale), so one list could sort one way on screen and another in an email. `naturalOrder` sat in the API's server-only `_base`, where the site could not import it (#3331).

**What changed**
- **`naturalOrder` and `byName` move** to `libs/gisday/common/src/lib/names.ts`, exported. `_base/natural-order.ts` is deleted and its two importers use the new home.
- **The ten sorts use `naturalOrder`:** the dashboards' table, Speaker badges, the site's speakers and presenters, the email composer's recipients, a judge's entries and the judging results, the judging service's judges and entries, and the season winners.

**Which lists change order, and how** (made-up examples):
- **Numbers in a name now sort as numbers** where they did not: speakers, presenters, email recipients, a judge's entries, the judging results and the season winners. "Team 2" now comes before "Team 10"; before, "Team 10" came first.
- **The judging service's judges** used the default `localeCompare`, which tells case apart. "ana" and "Ana" now tie, and keep their order.
- **The dashboards' table and Speaker badges** used the browser's language; now always `'en'`. For English names the same order.
