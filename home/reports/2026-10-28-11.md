## What the pictures show, and what I'd ship

**Option B at 25%, for both.**

| | Fort Worth Law School | Houston Space Institute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before | pink grid on solid white, four parcels | the whole site covered in a pink grid |
| A, fill hidden | **an empty black outline** at each parcel | an outline around the site; its buildings and roads still draw inside |
| B at 25% | a light orange wash; street names show through | the site marked, with B500, roads and parking all legible |

- **B draws inside the basemap's own style**, as the director expected: under the outline, labels, roads and buildings. There's no
  added layer, so there's no outline to break and no feature source to find.
- **Could either look worse than the pink to someone who doesn't know what's there? A could, at Fort Worth Law.** The top-left
  parcel becomes an empty box over a rail yard, and the two at the bottom become empty outlines on grass. To a visitor that reads
  as a missing or broken building, not a construction site. At Houston Space Institute A reads fine, because the site's
  buildings and roads fill the outline. B at 25% can't be mistaken for missing data: it says "something here", which is the job.
- **25% rather than 45%:** 45% turns all of Houston Space Institute's site into a solid orange block, louder than any building on
  the map. 25% marks it and keeps the campus readable. At Fort Worth Law both work; 25% is the one that suits both campuses.
- **This is a flat wash, not a hatch.** If the maintainer wants a hatch at the satellite campuses too, each campus's construction
  feature layer exists (above) and #2459's approach would carry over.

## The code (option B, on the branch)

- **`applyStyleLayerChanges(layer, { hiddenStyleLayers, styleLayerPaint })`** in
  `libs/maps/esri/src/lib/services/map/basemap-layers.ts`. Once the style loads, it hides and repaints style layers by id, with
  `setStyleLayerVisibility` and `setPaintProperties`. These are style calls only, and a failure only warns.
  - **`makeBaseLayer`** now uses it.
  - **The map service's `vector-tile` layer sources** use it too. That is how the campus basemaps are declared, so this was the
    missing half: #2459's `hiddenStyleLayers` worked only for basemap base layers.
- **The two options are declared on each basemap's own definition**, with nothing campus-specific in the code:
  - `fort-worth-law.definitions.ts` repaints `Construction/1`, and `houston-space-institute.definitions.ts` repaints
    `GIS.FCOR.HSI_Construction/1`, both with `styleLayerPaint: { '<fill>': { 'fill-color': 'rgba(230, 152, 0, 0.25)' } }`;
  - beside each is a comment giving option A as the one-line alternative, `hiddenStyleLayers: ['<fill>']`.

  Whichever the maintainer picks, the other is just a comment to delete.
- **Types:**
  - `VectorTileLayerSourceProperties` (`libs/common/types`) gains `hiddenStyleLayers` and `styleLayerPaint`;
  - the base-layer `LayerProperties` gains `styleLayerPaint`;
  - `StyleLayerChanges` is the one shape both use.
