## What I changed: each layer is asked with its own filter

The grid chose features with a plain query object. ArcGIS sends that without the layer's `definitionExpression`
(#1954), so on a filtered layer the grid could choose a feature the map does not draw, and count its miss as a click
that failed. That is the #2794 trap in its general form.

**The buildings layer was safe only by accident:** its filtered-out rows have no name, so they were skipped as unnamed.
Now every layer is asked from its own `createQuery()`.
