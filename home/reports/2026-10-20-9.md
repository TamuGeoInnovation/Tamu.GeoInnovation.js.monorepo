## 2. branch ready: `fix/1992-accessible-parking-sports-maps` (`35739faf2`, worktree `C:\TAMU\wt-1992`) - closes #1992 and #1993
- **Cause:** the renderers come from each map's **portal item**, not our definitions (the service itself publishes a black dot), and
  two are stale:

  | Map, layer | Renderer names | Features hold |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Women's Basketball, accessible | `Disabled,Volleyball` / `Disabled,Basketball` | `Accessible,Basketball` |
  | Indoor Track, accessible | `Disabled,Soccer` / `Disabled,Softball` (copied) | `Disabled,TrackIndoor` |
  | Indoor Track, Team Bus Parking (#1993) | `Team Drop Off` / `Team Bus Parking` | `SPORTS` |

  Every other map's accessible layer was surveyed: Volleyball, Soccer, Swimming, Softball match their data; Outdoor Track holds none.
- **Fix:** a new optional `LayerSource.symbolFromServiceClass: '<class label>'`. `applyPortalItemSymbology` then uses a simple renderer
  with that class's own symbol from the portal item or service - rather than pasting the 7 KB CIM wheelchair symbol, with its
  embedded image, into two files. A class the service no longer has leaves the layer as published. Used for 'Accessible Parking' on
  both maps and 'Team Bus Parking' on Indoor Track, with reasons beside each; Indoor Track's accessible layer also filters
  `type IN ('Accessible','Disabled')`.
- **Red** (production): Women's Basketball `Accessible Parking Spaces: none of the 2 features it holds has a symbol` and
  `listed with 0 rows and no symbol`; Indoor Track `Team Bus Parking: none of the 1 features…`, `Accessible Parking Spaces: none of

  shared libraries - **the full affected check here too: exit 0** (lint 38, test 36, build 5).
