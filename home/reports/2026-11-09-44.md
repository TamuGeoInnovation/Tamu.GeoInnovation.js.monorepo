## The decision: how production reaches the network

**`routingNetworkUrl`, the network on Transportation's host for the environment (`tsgisHost`)**, replaces the
development-only `developmentRoutingBaseUrl`, and the connection service uses it everywhere. Two other options were not
taken:

- **No discovery change.** Discovery stays only for an application that names no network: production's `Routing`
  folder is empty, and development's holds networks from 2022 that discovery would choose.
- **No move by the GIS team** is needed.
