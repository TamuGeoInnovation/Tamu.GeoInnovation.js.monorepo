## Where I disagree with #3355, and why

1. **Nature Rx is not "already filed": it's tonight's build.** PR 3281 (`e7008a499`, #3223) renamed the category to "Nature Spaces" and the popup's line to the space's name, and did not update `search-sources.spec.ts`. So tonight's build caused **two** of the 18 failures, not one (#3350 and this). Confirmed in the code. None of #2631, #2632, #2716 and #2723 covers it.
2. **The `basemapUrl` 400s are not four broken layers.** Every leaf layer of `FCOR/TAMU_Basemap_20261008`, 11 rounds: **7 of 638 count queries refused** (about 1.1%), each `400 Unable to complete operation.`, each on a **different** layer (0, 3, 5, 27, 38, 42, 45). The four named never failed tonight and answer `returnIdsOnly` at once. It's one service read by every environment, so production behaves the same. That's #2616 exactly; tonight's measurement is now on it. **No `environments.json` entry:** one for the whole service would hide whichever layer fails next.
3. **The empty filters are not "a definition-or-data question": both are right, and dev's layout differs.**
   - Dev publishes the three services split by category: BusinessParking layers 1 (90) and 2 "Only UB+" (7); ContractorParking 0 (6) and 1 (69); Freshman 0 (12) and 1 (24).
   - Production has one combined layer each, which the definitions filter. On production every filter selects its lots (90/7, 69/6, 12/24); on dev one of each pair selects 0.
   - The test is right that dev draws those layers empty. Not the #1961/#2500 shape, and no filter was changed.
4. **`popup.spec.ts:261` is not timing: it fails every time, on both environments.** The popup did open on Evans first and did repaint. The second click landed on another Evans Library feature (0468.1), because the link now frames at zoom 19.
5. **`layer-order` on indoor-track-parking is not "the only real one".** It passed 9 of 9 alone, dev and production, and the definition and test agree. Every layer-order failure seen was the 90 s draw-settle timeout; muster's failure screenshot shows the map fully drawn. The release run's own indoor-track error text isn't on this machine, so "timeout" is strongly indicated, not proven.

Agreed with #3355: the Lot 100g failures are performance, not popups. They take 25 to 73 s to finish drawing on dev and production alike, against 19 s for `/map/d`, all of it after the last network request.
