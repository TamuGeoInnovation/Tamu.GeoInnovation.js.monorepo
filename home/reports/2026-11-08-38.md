## The decision: the section decides the address

`mapSegment(discover)` in `ts-events-ngx` gives a map's first segment. **A map whose `mapType`/`mapTypes` includes
`transportation` is at `/transportation`, whatever its `type`.** Every other map is where it always was.

**Why the section, rather than giving these maps `type: 'transportation'`:**
- **A new `type` would leave two properties that must agree.** You can still set `mapType: 'transportation'` with
  `type: 'parking'`, which is exactly the fault. Deriving the address from the section makes disagreement impossible
  by construction, not by a test.
- **`type` means something else too.** `EventService` reads it to decide whether a map loads the main map's layers
  (`kiosk`, `satellite-campus`). The two Transportation maps are `general-map`s like the parking maps and should keep
  that behaviour. Changing their `type` would mean auditing every reader of it, for no gain.

**Where `mapSegment` is used.** One function serves both sides, so they cannot drift apart:
- **The All Maps links** (`getApplicationRoute`).
- **`MapAddressGuard`, on every event map's route.** A Transportation map reached at any other segment moves to
  `/transportation/<id>`, keeping the rest of the address. So `/parking/micromobility/map/d?zoom=17` becomes
  `/transportation/micromobility/map/d?zoom=17`.

Only Transportation maps move. A parking map's `/events/<id>`, which has always worked, is left alone, and no other
`/parking/` map is touched.

**The route.** `/transportation` is mounted in `aggiemap.routes.ts` beside the other five, loading the same
`tsEventsRoutes`.

**The smoke suite follows:**
- `DEVELOPMENT_ONLY_SERVICES`' Micromobility page is now `/transportation/micromobility`.
- `global-setup.ts`'s crawl reads `/transportation/` links and addresses.
- The development-only map check opens the new addresses. For a moved map it also checks the old `/parking/<id>`:
  on production it lands on `/map`, and elsewhere it moves to the new address.
- `DEVELOPMENT_ONLY_CONTROLS` needed nothing: none of its controls links to these two maps.
