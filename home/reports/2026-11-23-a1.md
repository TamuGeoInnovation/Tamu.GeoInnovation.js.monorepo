## What it is

`test/smoke/aggiemap/outbound-links.spec.ts`, with `outbound-links.ts` beside it.

**The links,** collected from where they're written:
- every event definition's `scheduleUrl`;
- the app's own links: `href`s in `libs/aggiemap` and `libs/maps/feature` templates, and addresses in their code.

Excluded: services and APIs (`arcgis/rest` in any case, `/api/`, the GIS hosts), `aggiemap.tamu.edu` itself, templated
addresses, examples, specs and the Code Maroon feed's saved copy. **31 links today.**

**All seven rules from the issue and its comment:**
1. Loaded in a Playwright page, as a browser does.
2. A redirect passes, and its annotation says where it landed.
3. Judged by the page:
   - a 404 or 410 with a real page fails;
   - a 200 whose page says "not found" fails;
   - a 5xx fails;
   - a 401, 403 or 429 or a timeout is inconclusive, never red.
4. Two strikes, 30 seconds apart.
5. `allowedOutboundLinkFailures` in `environments.json` (development and production, empty). When a listed link works
   again, the test says to remove it.
6. A desktop Chrome user agent and `Accept-Language` on the context.
7. A 404 whose body is a storage error or next to nothing is inconclusive.

**Seen to fail:** a test points the check at `https://aggie.tamu.edu/graduation` directly, a fixture rather than a
re-broken definition, and it is called **dead**. A live schedule link is called working. The verdict rules have their
own tests, without the network.

**Not in the quick tier:** nothing in `scope.ts` names it, so it runs in release and full.
