Since #2667 (6 November), a development build reads Transportation's development services (`gis.dev.tamu.edu/.../TS`).
Production reads `arc.ts.tamu.edu/.../TS_Events`. Two specs still assume production's:

| Test | What happened | Shown by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `parking-spaces.spec`: `?lot=100j` selects a motorcycle space | **The app is right.** It selected space 15548, and dev's service says that is `100j`, `M/C`. The spec asks `Connections`, which under Node is production's host, where 15548 does not exist. So `LotName` came back undefined | querying 15548 on both servers |
| `directions.spec`: the event-map popup offers "Directions To Here" (desktop and phone) | `?feature=AVP-parking-lots:26135` opened no popup. **26135 is production's object id for Lot 043**; dev's AnyValidPermit service has no feature 26135 | querying 26135 on both servers |

fail on every run until the specs ask the environment's own host.** The other `directions.spec` tests, on the main
map and the tailgate tent, passed.

### The tailgating kiosk now draws restrooms (#2661): 1 failure

"`/kiosk/football-tailgating/map` also loads the main map's layers: `tailgating-restrooms`."

- **The message is misleading.** `tailgating-restrooms` is the tailgating map's own layer, added by #2661.

  `sources == [...TailgatingColdLayerSources, buildings]`.
- **The fix:** add it to `kiosk.spec.ts`'s `TAILGATING_KIOSK_LAYER_IDS`.

### The 3D test clicks a basemap that 3D disables: 1 failure, 45 minutes

`perspective.spec`'s "3D is a globe ... on every campus basemap" timed out at 15 minutes, three times.

- **What it was waiting on:** a click on "Aggieland vector, September 2026" while in 3D.
- **Why that click never lands:**
  - That basemap is `Hosted/VTBase` in State Plane (wkid 32139), which a scene cannot draw.
  - Since #1746 the gallery marks it `aria-disabled`, "Not available in 3D", and ignores the click.
  - Playwright waited the whole 15 minutes for the button to enable.
- **Dev only:** production's gallery offers one basemap, in Web Mercator, so the test has nothing else to click there.
- **The fix:** the spec should skip a basemap the gallery marks unavailable, and assert the mark rather than click it.
  The test would also be fairer with a short click timeout: 45 minutes of a 72-minute run went on it.
