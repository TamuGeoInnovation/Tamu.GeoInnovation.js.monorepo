## Found on the way: Angulartics tracks page views nowhere

- **AggieMap:** the gtag provider sends page views only to `settings.gst.trackingIds`. No app configures them, and the default only fills them
  from analytics.js trackers, which AggieMap does not have. So Angulartics' page tracking is a no-op. AggieMap's page views come only from
  `index.html`'s `gtag('config', …)`, and from GA4's own history tracking if the property has enhanced measurement on. **Events do work**
  (`gtag('event', …)` needs no ids), which is what the smoke check proves.
- **GIS Day** pairs a gtag snippet with the analytics.js provider, whose `ga()` does not exist on the page. **Angulartics sends nothing at all**:
  no page views, no events.
- **UES operations and GIS Day competitions** report to **`UA-` Universal Analytics properties**, which Google has retired. UES also loads a
  GA4 tag (`G-4WSTL5S2HP`).

None of this changed with 14, and none of it is this batch's to fix.
