## 1. The workflow's tags: what the front ends move to

`.github/workflows/build-publish.yaml` runs **on every `push`, on any branch** (`on: push`, no filter) and pushes to
`ghcr.io/tamugeoinnovation/nginx-base` with `docker/metadata-action`:

| Tag | When |
| --- | --- |
| **`sha-<7-character short sha>`** | every push |
| `<branch name>` (`type=ref,event=branch`) | every push, e.g. `master` |
| `latest` | only when the branch is `master` (`flavor: latest=${{ github.ref_name == 'master' }}`) |
| `pr-<n>` (`type=ref,event=pr`) | **never**: the workflow has no `pull_request` trigger |

**So after the director's PR merges to `master`,** the four Dockerfiles in the monorepo move from `nginx-base:sha-09b7e41` to
**`nginx-base:sha-<the merge commit's first 7>`**:

- `apps/gisday-angular/src/Dockerfile`
- `apps/geoservices-angular/src/Dockerfile`
- `apps/mailroom-angular/src/Dockerfile`

That tag exists once that merge's run finishes. A merge commit gets its own SHA; a squash or rebase merge gives a different one again. Either
way it is the SHA on `master` after the merge, **not `56e83f6`**.
