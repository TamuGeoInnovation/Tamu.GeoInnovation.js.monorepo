### The difference on #2474: UTC, not -06:00

What `vgi_seasons` held after the save:

```
scoringOpensAt   2026-11-14 06:00:00.0000000 +00:00
scoringClosesAt  2026-11-20 17:00:00.0000000 +00:00     (DATEPART(TZOFFSET) = 0)
```

- **The moments are right:** 06:00 UTC is 00:00 CST, and 17:00 UTC is 11:00 CST.
- **The batch expected `-06:00`.** The Designer sends `toISOString()`, which is UTC, and the API stores it as it comes.
- **Nothing reads the offset**, as far as I can see. Scoring compares instants, and the Designer shows Central time whatever the
  offset is. It's question 1 below.
