## In short

**The tile draws anyway, and that changes what #2702 claims. But a failed buildings tile leaves a square of the map where no building
can be clicked, until the page is reloaded.**

- **Nothing goes missing on screen.** `buildings-layer` draws with a fully transparent fill, and `surface-lots-layer` has opacity 0.
  The buildings and lots a visitor sees come from the vector tile basemap, which doesn't use these queries.
- **The proof:** a load where a lots tile failed, panned so the failed tile sits in the middle, and a clean load panned to the same
  place differ by **0 pixels** (`failed-lots-tile-panned-in.png`, `clean-load-same-place.png`).
- **What a failed query does lose is the click:**
  - **Inside a failed buildings tile, no building opened its popup: 0 of 24 clicks in 8 loads.** That includes buildings the layer view
    still partly holds from a neighbouring tile.
  - **`view.hitTest` there finds no feature layer at all**, not just the missing building.
  - **On the same loads, buildings outside the failed tile opened theirs: 7 of 8.**
  - **On clean loads, the same tile's buildings opened theirs: 2 of 2.**
- **It doesn't heal:** panning the failed tile into view never asked for it again, in 3 of 3 loads. The layer view stays short until
  a reload.
- **How often:**
  - 8 of 36 production loads (22%) lost a buildings tile in the two click runs;
  - 6 of 20 in the capture runs lost a buildings, lots or layer-2 tile;
  - 5 of 10 in batch 10.
- **This ties #2702 to #2707.** For a visitor, the dead square is "clicking a building sometimes opens nothing", and it lasts for the
  whole visit, not just the first seconds that report 11 measured.

## What was done

**The capture** (`tile.cjs`):
- **Loads:** fresh contexts of production's `/map/d`, at 1280×950, recording every query to `TAMU_Basemap_20261008` layers 1, 2 and
  9, with each one's tile envelope.
- **When one failed:**
  - waited for drawing to finish, then photographed the map and outlined the failed tile;
  - compared the layer view's feature count in the tile's envelope with the service's;
  - hid the sidebar and the notification with CSS and panned the tile into the middle at the same zoom (`goTo`, no animation);
  - waited for drawing again, photographed it, and recounted;
  - recorded every query the pan caused.

**The clicks** (`tileclick.cjs`):
- **Loads:** further production loads, until a `buildings-layer` (layer 1) query failed.
- **The pan:** the failed tile's middle went to the middle of the part of the map the sidebar doesn't cover, with nothing hidden.
- **Each failed load, five buildings clicked by `view.toScreen` of their centroid:**
  - one inside the tile that the layer view still holds;
  - two inside it that the view lost;
  - two outside the tile, as controls.
- **For each click:** what `view.hitTest` found at that point, and whether the right popup opened within 8 s.

**Run 1:** the same, without the outside controls, on 4 failed loads out of 17. Inside the tile, **0 of 12** (0 of 4 kept, 0 of 8 lost).

**What `hitTest` found:** in every one of the 24 inside clicks it found no feature layer at that point, only the basemap's vector tiles.
For 7 of the 8 outside clicks it found `buildings-layer`.

**The miss outside:** Kyle Field, in load 18. Its centroid is within reach of the tile's edge, and its `hitTest` was empty like the
inside ones.

**The control on clean loads:** I took a tile envelope that failed in run 2 and ran the same steps on 2 loads where nothing failed.
- **The view's count:** 23 of 25 buildings in that envelope, so a gap of about 2 is normal.
- **On failed loads,** the same envelope held 8 to 11 of 25.
- **Clicks:** the building inside opened its popup, 2 of 2.

**Not the whole map:** a separate check (`failclick.cjs`) clicked the opening view's two buildings from report 11 after drawing. On
loads where a buildings tile failed elsewhere, both still opened, 4 of 4. It then crashed on my own query to the service, which hit
the same 500.

**Why the whole tile goes dead, as I read it:**
- **What the measurements show:** every building in the tile is unclickable, including ones the view still counts.
- **The explanation:** the layer view keeps each tile's features clipped to that tile. A building that also falls in a neighbouring
  tile is still counted in the envelope, but there's nothing in the failed tile's own area for `hitTest` to find.
- **What I haven't checked:** Esri's internals.

## Does it heal?

**No.**
- **After the pan,** the map asked for 9 to 15 new tiles each time, all for other tiles. **It never asked for the failed tile again**,
  in 3 of 3 loads. The view's count in the envelope stayed short: lots 8 to 11 of 23, buildings 11 of 25 and 10 of 31.
- **Panning even made a new one:** on one load a different buildings tile failed during the pan itself.
- **So the dead square stays for the visit.** I didn't test whether zooming in or out and back recovers it.

## What this changes in #2702

- **#2702 can stop saying the map "draws short".** It draws everything. What's short is the click layer.
- **The failure matters more than a missing tile would:**
  - **It's invisible**, so nobody reports it as a data problem.
  - **It's permanent for the visit.**
  - **It lands on a fifth to a half of production loads:** about 1.5% of requests failing, and 36 requests per load.
- **The same fault breaks search highlights and anything else that asks the layer view** for features in that area. I measured only
  clicks.
- **What the GIS team should be handed:** the 500 rate from report 10, plus a dead click square in roughly one load in five.
