  - Which one crashed I cannot say: the core dump went with the container.
- **Why load, if it is load:** a native module shutting down while its own threads are still busy is the usual cause of a crash at exit, and
  it gets likelier when the CPU is short. That is a reasoned guess, not a measurement.
