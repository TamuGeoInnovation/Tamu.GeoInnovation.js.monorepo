## Which toggles go grey, on deployed dev today

I read each toggle's classes and attributes on `dev.aggiemap.tamu.edu` (`21e69426e`):

| Trip | Walking | Biking | Driving | Bus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `stops=30.6150,-96.3500@30.6150,-96.3300` (the release note's) | active, 35 min | 35 min | 7 min | 30 min |
| the same, with **Accessible Friendly Directions** on | active, 36 min | **`disabled`** | 7 min | **`disabled`** |
| `stops=30.6142,-96.3420@30.6100,-96.3404` | active, 7 min | 8 min | **`error`**, "--" | **`error`**, "--" |
| `stops=30.6040,-96.3550@30.6190,-96.3390` | active, 31 min | **`error`**, "--" | **`error`**, "--" | **`error`**, "--" |

**So the grey state you can reach today** is the issue's own case: the options exclude the mode. The network has no accessible
bike or bus mode, so with accessible directions on, Biking and Bus are `isModeRestricted`.

**A "No route" mode is a different state.** It was asked for and answered with an error, so it has a result and isn't grey: it has
the `error` class, shows "--", and stays clickable. That's the case copilot-home saw. The batch's second condition, results back
with none for this toggle, doesn't come from "No route"; it covers a mode that was never asked for.

**What a click on the grey Biking did before the fix:** nothing you could see. The address bar and `aria-pressed` stayed put,
because `calculateTravelMode` finds no mode the options allow and changes nothing. **The fault was everything else:**
- it was a live `role="button"`, with no `aria-disabled`, so a screen reader announced it as an ordinary button;
- it was in the tab order, **with no visible focus when Tab landed on it** (the before picture);
- its handlers still called the planner.

With a mode the options allow but which has no result yet, the click would have switched mode. The unit test covers that case.
