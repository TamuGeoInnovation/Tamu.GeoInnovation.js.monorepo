## 2. The change

- **Where the address lives.** The GIS Day library cannot import AggieMap's connections without depending on AggieMap. And the address was
  already written out **three times**: AggieMap's `connections.ts` (`basemapUrl`), its `basemaps.definition.ts` (the production tile layer),
  and `ues-operations-angular`'s `definitions.ts`. So it is now **`TAMU_CAMPUS_BASEMAP_URL`** in **`@tamu-gisc/common/datasets/geographic`**,
  and **all four read it**: those three and `libs/maps/mapbox`.
  - **Why there:** that library holds plain geographic data with no dependencies (the US states dataset), so importing it pulls neither
    Esri nor MapLibre code into anyone's bundle, and it is already shared across apps.
  - **When AggieMap's campus basemap is republished, one line moves both.**
- **The default style** in `libs/maps/mapbox`: a MapLibre style with one raster source (`${TAMU_CAMPUS_BASEMAP_URL}/tile/{z}/{y}/{x}`, 256
  px, maxzoom 23) and one raster layer, credited *"Texas A&M University"*. OpenFreeMap is gone from the code.

**What shows beyond campus at the pages' zooms (14 to 16):** the service's own tiles cover the whole of Bryan and College Station, with the
surrounding streets drawn in light grey and labelled. So at the pages' view (centre −96.3407, 30.6158) there is no empty area. Panning far
past Bryan or College Station would show MapLibre's empty background.
