## copilot-home's blank-map check (`test/2487-blank-map-smoke`)

The check isn't flaky. It does 6 separate loads with no retries, and a healthy map draws in a few seconds, so a healthy build never
fails it.

**It is a sample, though, and it catches only a fault that blanks most loads for certain.** How likely 6 loads are to catch at
least one blank:
- **at this rig's rate tonight, about 1 in 7:** about 60%;
- **at batch 163's 4 of 15:** about 84%;
- **at copilot-home's 1 of 18:** about 30%.

So it would have caught #2481, which blanked every load, every time. On the current build it catches the fault some nights and not
others, depending on the machine.

**Recommended:** keep it as it is, in the release tier. Its pass means "no fault that blanks most loads". Raise
`AGGIEMAP_SMOKE_BLANK_LOADS` to about 20 when clearing a change to the basemap. A red result is always real.
