## #2716: the carousel

**What I tried first, and why it isn't the fix:**
- **`loading="lazy"` saved nothing.** With it on every slide past the first, ILCB Green Space still fetched all four photographs.
- **The reason:** the next slides sit at x = 1264 to 1920 in a 1280-wide window. That's within the distance at which Chrome loads a lazy
  image anyway. Swiper's own lazy support relies on exactly that attribute.

**The fix, behind the same interface:**
- **The first photo is fetched at once.** A further slide's `<img>` has no `src` until the slide is reached. Reaching one fetches it and
  the one either side, round the loop.
- **Two details needed care:**
  - **Swiper's layout reports `slidechange` twice more after initializing, still on the first slide.** That fetched three photos on
    opening until I made a change that doesn't move the carousel fetch nothing.
  - **A bound `null` `src` came out as `src=""` in the unit tests.** I didn't want to rely on the browser treating that as no request,
    so an unreached slide's `<img>` is written with no `src` attribute at all.
- **Checked live on the final code:** moving next, back, and back again round the loop showed ILCB2, ILCB1 and ILCB4, each loaded.
- **The spec:** `photo-carousel.component.spec.ts` gets 5 tests: 8 of 8 pass, and **3 fail on the old component**.
- **Who gains:** the Points of Interest and Bonfire popups, which use the same component; nothing outside AggieMap's popups uses it.

**What lazy fetching alone is worth, measured** (photographs fetched in the 20 s after the popup opens, Playwright, 1280 × 950):

| | Before (dev's build) | After (this branch, local) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ILCB Green Space, 4 photographs, on opening | 4 requests, **6,873,892 bytes** | 1 request, **1,858,809 bytes** |
| The same, after moving one slide | 4, 6,873,892 bytes | 3, 5,079,132 bytes |
| West Campus Spaces, 1 photograph | 1, 1,133,659 bytes | 1, 1,133,712 bytes |

- **Who saves:**
  - **a visitor who opens ILCB Green Space and looks at one photograph downloads 5.0 MB less (73%)**;
  - one who looks at them all downloads the same as before;
  - a space with one photograph gains nothing.
- **It doesn't rescue the popup on its own:** **the first photograph, 1.86 MB, is the floor** until the files are resized. That's #2716's
  other half, with Facilities Analytics & Mapping, along with the ten listed photographs not on the server. I didn't touch those.
