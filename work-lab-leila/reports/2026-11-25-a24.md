## 3. What I conclude

1. **On this machine, both maps finish drawing every time,** and quickly. Nothing here is a map that never finishes.
2. **This machine can't tell a budget from a bug,** because it never gets near the budget, even at 100% CPU. #3369 notes
   that the slow part is the view updating after its data has arrived, on headless Chromium's software WebGL. That is CPU
   work, so it scales with the machine. This one has 24 logical processors; the release tiers that failed ran elsewhere.
3. **The evidence that does bear on the budget is #3369's own:** break-summer took up to **72.9 s** after `ready`, alone, on
   23 November. That's past `popup.spec.ts`'s **60 s** drawing wait on its own, before any load.
   - **It means:** on that machine, the popup check can fail with nothing else running. That's a budget too small for that
     machine, not a map that never finishes.
4. **What I would change, as its own small pull request:** not this batch, and only after the next step. Give `popup.spec.ts`'s
   and `building-clicks.spec.ts`'s drawing wait the same 90 s as `layer-order` and `selection`. Then measure once on the
   machine that failed. And make `layer-order`'s timeout message say which condition timed out, as #3369 itself proposes.
