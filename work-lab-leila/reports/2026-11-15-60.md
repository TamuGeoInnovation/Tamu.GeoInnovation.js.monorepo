## What I saw while capturing

1. **The table cuts off its right-hand columns at 1280 px, and they can't be scrolled to.** `ngx-datatable` has
   `overflow-x: hidden`.
   - **RSVPs:** content 926 px wide in 879 px; most of "Not given" is hidden. This is also on `development`, so it's
     older.
   - **Check-ins (new):** content 982 px wide in 879 px. Hidden: "Not given", part of "Checked in", and, in an opened
     event, **Export as CSV** and the check-in times.
   - **The images show it as it is.**
2. **The "accounts could not be read" count isn't filtered by site.** `siteDashboard()` filters the events and the
   counts, but passes `unreadAccounts` through unchanged. So on gisday.tamu.edu it reads:
   - "3197 RSVPs for 60 events. 3924 accounts could not be read";
   - "1591 check-ins … 1740 accounts could not be read".

   In both, there are more unread accounts than records.
3. **The site filter runs in the browser.** The API still sends gisday.tamu.edu every organization's events and counts.
   These are counts, not names, so it's worth knowing but isn't a privacy problem.
