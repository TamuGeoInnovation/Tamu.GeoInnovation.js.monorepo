`template-parser@22.6.0`. All of 22.6.0 is consistent with itself (batch 36 checked: `angular-eslint@22.6.0` depends on each
`@angular-eslint/*` at exactly `22.6.0`). The trouble is that the eight packages can only move **together**, and npm will not
