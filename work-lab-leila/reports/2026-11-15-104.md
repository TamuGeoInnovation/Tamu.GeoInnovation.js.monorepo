## What changed

- **The cap:** `DashboardScrollComponent` has a new `capped` input.
  - **What it does:** a capped box is at most `calc(100vh - 8rem)` tall, scrolls inside itself, and keeps its own scrollbar
    rather than the rail.
  - **Short lists:** because it's a `max-height`, a short list is only as tall as its people.
- **Where it applies:** `DashboardPeopleComponent` sets `[capped]="true"`, so every opened row is capped, on RSVPs,
  Check-ins, Accounts and VGI. The dashboard table's own box isn't capped, and keeps its rail.
- **Why 8rem:** the row's name and "Export as CSV" take about 90 px above the box. 8rem keeps the name, the whole box and its
  scrollbar in one window at 1280 × 900.
- **The Email column is 190 px, from 200:** a capped box's vertical scrollbar takes 15 px, so every long RSVP list grew a
  sideways scrollbar for 9 px. The six columns now total 800.
- **Where I looked:** `libs/ui-kits/ngx/**`, `libs/sass/` and the dashboards' shared pieces. The only sticky header is in
  `_tables.scss` (`floating-table`), which the people table doesn't use. So none was added (question 1).
