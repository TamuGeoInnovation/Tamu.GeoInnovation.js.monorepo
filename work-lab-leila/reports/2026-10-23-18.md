## The entry

Added to the end of **Things that look broken** in `docs/gisday-local.md`, in the shape of its neighbours (symptom, why,
what to do):

- the error, and that it happens even with `AUTH0_MANAGEMENT_DISABLED=true`;
- why: only the local build reads that switch (the start-up log shows `disabled: undefined`), and the production
  configuration requires the three values, checking only that they are there, with no network call at start-up;
- the three placeholder lines, and that anything reading or changing Auth0 users then fails when used, not at start-up;
- **and one sentence the batch did not ask for:** run by hand rather than through `scripts/gisday-local.sh`, the image also
  needs `TYPEORM_PASSWORD`, or it stops earlier with `Missing db environment variables`. That is the first thing anyone
  following the entry would hit, as I did;
- a link to [#2133](https://github.com/TamuGeoInnovation/Tamu.GeoInnovation.js.monorepo/issues/2133).
