## The fields

These are the ones the three entities, and the records nested in them, declare as `Date`:
- **Every record:** `created` and `updated`.
- **`SeasonDay`:** `date`.
- **`Season`:** the eight competition deadlines (`SUBMISSION_DEADLINE_FIELDS`).
- **`SeasonCompetition`:** `finalistsNotifiedAt`.

`Event` has no `Date` field of its own beyond these; a session's dates are its `day.date` and its timestamps.

## The shape

**`fromWire(body)` copies the response** and turns each of those fields into a `Date`, at any depth, when it holds an
ISO instant.
- **Not converted:** a `null` or missing date, a time of day (`startTime: '14:00:00'`), and every other field. They come
  back as they came.
- **`withWireDates()`** is the same, as an rxjs operator.

**The wiring:**
- **`BaseService`** takes `{ wireDates: true }` and sends its ten requests through one protected `answer()`. The other
  services are unchanged, since the option is off by default.
- **The three services** turn it on and send their own 8 entity requests through `answer()`:
  - `SeasonService`: `getActiveSeason`, `getSeasonByYear`, create, update and delete;
  - `SeasonDayService`: `getDayEvents`;
  - `EventService`: `getEvent` and `getEventsByDay`.
- **Left as they are:** the requests for counts, attendance, the active flag, and the deadline and competition setters.
  None of their responses carries an entity's date.

**Reuse:** it's one converter, in the library's `_base` beside `noSeasonAs`, behind the base service's existing
interface. No page changes how it calls a service.
