## The removal that was wrong: `@eslint/js`

Batch 149 marked `@eslint/js` **certain**: no config imports it, and nothing in the lock depends on it or lists it as a peer. **The full check
proved that wrong.** On the cold "after" run, lint failed for 101 of 104 projects:

```
Error: Cannot find module '@eslint/js'
Require stack:
- /w/node_modules/@nx/eslint-plugin/dist/src/flat-configs/typescript.js
```

`@nx/eslint-plugin` requires `@eslint/js` without declaring it, in neither its dependencies nor its peers, so only the root's entry makes it
resolvable. I restored it at `^10.0.1`, as before, in its own commit (`21a4f0bea`) that says why; the lock adds back exactly that one package.
Nothing was pushed before this was caught.

**Batch 149's table is corrected by this:** `@eslint/js` is **used**, through `@nx/eslint-plugin`. `@eslint/eslintrc`, from the same commit,
passed lint without it.
