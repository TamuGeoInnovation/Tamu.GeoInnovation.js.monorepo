### Why: the dev views are a different schema

Each view's fields read from both servers' REST (`C:\TAMU\b15\ath-fields.jsonl` has every layer's field list):

- **Event parking lots** (every map): production's layer carries the per-sport columns (`baseball`, `softball`, `soccer`, `trackxc`,
  `lottype` and ~45 more). **Dev's carries none of them**, but a generic set instead: `event`, `type`, `name`, `description`,
  `startdate`, `enddate`, `notes`, and marker-feed fields (`mrkid`, `feed`, `popup`, `schedule` ...).
- **Sublayer order is shifted** on most maps. For example, soccer's dev `/0` is *Accessible Parking Spaces* and `/1` *Soccer Event Parking
  Lots*, where production's `/0` is *Purchase hourly visitor parking*, `/1` *Accessible Parking Space* and `/2` the lots. #2176 had
  already re-pointed the lots, which is why they draw.
- **Accessible Parking Spaces**: the definitions filter `event = '<Sport>' AND type IN ('Accessible', 'Disabled')`. **Dev's accessible
  layer has no `event` and no `type`** (production's has `x`, `y`, `type`).
- **Baseball** keeps production's old layer roles (`/0` symbols, `/2` closures, `/3` lots) and filters on `event`, `type` and `baseball`.
  Dev's layers have none of those columns.
- **Cross country's** lots filter `trackxc = 'EventParking'`, a column dev's view no longer has.

**So this needs a decision between the GIS team and the code**: either the dev views get the columns the definitions use (`event` and
`type` on the accessible layer, the per-sport columns or an equivalent on the lots, `baseball`/`event`/`type` on baseball's layers), or the
definitions are rewritten to the new schema. The GIS team should not publish these views to production as they are: production's
definitions would then fail the same way.
