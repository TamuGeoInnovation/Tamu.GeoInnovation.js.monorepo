### What AggieMap's triggers are, and why the change is CSS transitions

**All three are state triggers** (`state('true')`, `state('false')`, `transition('* => *')`): each element stays on the page and moves between two
states. **`animate.enter`/`animate.leave` only apply to elements entering or leaving the DOM**, so they do not fit these. The faithful
replacement is a **CSS transition between two states**, with each trigger's own duration, delay and easing. (The commit uses the batch's
message.) The batch's other suggestion, CSS transitions, is what fits. The remaining apps' triggers include true enter and leave animations
(toasts, `inOutAnimation`), where `animate.enter`/`animate.leave` will be right.

- **`slide`:** `AbstractSlidingDrawerComponent` binds the host's `transform` from `visible` itself (`none`, or `translate(<width + offset>)`
  towards its side), in place of `@HostBinding('@slide')` and its params. The sidebar and drawer stylesheets include `slide-transition`. This
  shared base also moves geoservices' drawer and the other apps' sidebars, with the same movement.
- **`offCanvasSlideUpFromTop`:** `[class.off-canvas-hidden]` on the search bar and the trip planner's bar.
- **`offCanvasSlideInFromBottom`:** `[class.off-canvas-shown]` on the backdrop. Its `display` changes at the start of showing and the end of
  hiding, as the trigger's `sequence` did, using `transition-behavior: allow-discrete` (`display ... allow-discrete`) and `@starting-style`.
- **Reuse rule:** the easing (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 0, 0.25, 1)`, which every trigger used) and the three movements are mixins in **one new file,
  `libs/sass/_motion.scss`**, included by the four components.
- **Removed:**
  - the triggers' files (`ui-kits/ngx/layout/.../animations/drawer.ts` and its export; `aggiemap/ngx/ui/mobile/.../animations/elements.ts`);
  - the components' `animations: [...]`;
  - AggieMap's `BrowserAnimationsModule`;
  - `provideNoopAnimations()` from three specs that needed it only for these triggers (the sidebar's, the trip planner top's, the
    reveal-sidebar's). They now render with **no animations provider**, which is itself the proof that nothing they draw needs one.
- **`@angular/animations` stays**: the other apps' triggers still import it. The lock does not change.

## 2. Recorded before and after

The same script both times, on AggieMap's dev server: each movement is driven through its component (dev mode's `ng.getComponent`), and the
element's computed `transform`, `display` and `visibility` are sampled through the movement. All frames are in
`docs/screenshots/animate-enter-leave/frames-{before,after}.json`, with a screenshot of each end state beside them.

| Movement | Before: start → end | After: start → end |

- **Timing: the same range**, with each movement mid-way at a comparable time (for example, the backdrop at 515px of 812 at ~217 ms before and
  374px at ~228 ms after).
- **But the sampling is coarse:** on a page busy drawing the map, a 10 ms timer got only 4 to 9 readings per 600 ms movement, before and after
  alike. So the timelines pin the start and end states and roughly when each moves; they cannot compare frame by frame.
