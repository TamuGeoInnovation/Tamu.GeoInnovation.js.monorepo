## What the check does

**Where:** `development-only-gates.spec.ts` in `aggiemap-ngx-discover`, because it's the one library that sees all three
build-side sources:
- the main map's `DEVELOPMENT_ONLY_LAYER_SOURCES` (through `factory`);
- the event maps' `developmentOnly`;
- every Discover listing, `visible: false` included (through `DiscoveryService`, so #2743's main-map entries are covered
  when they arrive).

It reads the smoke suite's lists from `test/smoke/aggiemap/development-only.ts` itself, not a copy.

**It fails when:**

1. **A layer is left out of a production build but no `DEVELOPMENT_ONLY_SERVICES` entry watches its service.**
2. **A `developmentOnly` map's route isn't in the `pages` of the service entry for its layers**, so nothing loads it on
   production to prove the guard.
3. **A listing is hidden from production (`visible: false`) but the suite checks it nowhere.** Each needs one of: its
   route in a service's `pages`, a `DEVELOPMENT_ONLY_CONTROLS` link to it (**your bus entry's choice**), or a reason in
   the new `LISTINGS_HIDDEN_FROM_PRODUCTION`.
4. **A watched service is gated by nothing:** no excluded layer or development-only map reads it, and it declares no
   `runTimeGate`. Or it claims both, which means one is stale.
5. **A control links to a listing that isn't hidden, or a reason is kept for a listing that no longer is.**

## What it cannot cover (written in the spec and in `CLAUDE.md`)

- **A run-time gate is code, not a list.** `runTimeGate` is a declaration the test reads and can't verify. If someone
  deletes the `isTesting` in `CodeMaroonAlertComponent`, this test still passes. **The smoke run on production is what
  catches that**, and it remains the only check for those three services.
- **`DEVELOPMENT_ONLY_SECTIONS` (sidebar tabs) and `DEVELOPMENT_ONLY_ALL_MAPS_TILES` have no build side** to compare
  with, for the same reason: the tab and the tile are gated in templates.
- **Controls that aren't links to a map** (the dated raster basemaps in the gallery) are the same. Only a control whose
  selector names a route is compared, by its `href`.

So the check covers everything enumerable: every build-time gate, every listing, and the declarations for the rest. The
rest is stated rather than silently claimed.

## The side-panel issue: drafted, for you to file (this machine has no `gh`)

**Title:** `bug(maps-feature-trip-planner): Choosing a bus route fits it under the side panel, hiding its end`

**Type** Bug; **labels** `bug`; **project** Texas A&M Aggiemap. **Suggested priority: Low**, the maintainer to set.

> Choosing a bus route on the bus panel zooms the map to fit the route, but the fit uses the whole map view, which runs
> under the side panel. So the end of a route nearest the panel can sit behind it.
>
> Measured on dev's deployed build, 8 November 2026, at 1280 x 950, route 47 (RELLIS):
>
> - The map view is the full window, 1280 px wide, with **no padding** (`view.padding` is `{}`).
> - The side panel starts at **x = 934**.
> - After the fit, route 47's extent runs from x = 243 to **x = 1037**, so about 100 px of the route is under the panel.
>
> The cause: `BusService.toggleMapRoute` fits with `view.goTo(route_graphic)` (`bus.service.ts`, around line 800), which
> fits to the whole view. Passing the panel's width as padding, or setting `view.padding` while the panel is open, would
> fit the route into the part of the map a visitor can see.
>
> This is existing behaviour of the bus panel, the same from `/map`, not a regression. It was noticed while building the
> bus map (#2743).
