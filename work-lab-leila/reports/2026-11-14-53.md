## The layout, against the issue

- **Collapsible.** It starts collapsed when the season has any deadline, and open when it has none.
  - **Collapsed, it shows one line,** for example: "Poster closes Fri, Nov 13, 5:00 PM; files Mon, Nov 16, 12:00 PM ·
    Presentation open (Central time)".
  - **With no deadlines,** the line says "No deadlines set: every competition is open."
- **The toggle:** the whole title bar opens and closes it. The section's name is a real `<button>` with
  `aria-expanded`, so it works from the keyboard.
- **Compact:**
  - a table with one row per competition, with a column for when submissions close and one for when files close;
  - the Central time beside each picker, and a smaller Clear;
  - Save at the panel's end, on the right, at its own width, with the status message beside it.
- **Two competitions or four:** the rows and the summary come from `DEADLINE_COMPETITIONS`, so #2962's paper and
  cartography rows appear without changes.
