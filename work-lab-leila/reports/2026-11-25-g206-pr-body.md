Closes #3552

## What this changes

Angular's `date` pipe with no zone formats in the visitor's own zone. A season day is stored as midnight Central, so
west of Central every date showed as the day before: the home page and About told a visitor in Pacific time that GIS
Day ran 15 to 19 November when it ran 16 to 20. A shared `centralDate` pipe, in `gisday-platform-ngx-common`, formats
each instant at its own Central offset (CDT or CST), and replaces `date` and the fixed `'-0600'` in 26 templates.
