  > a newer build", not "the application is broken" (#3539).

- **It skips only on that positive answer.** With no tag to compare with, or a commit the clone lacks, or a tag that
  carries the change, the check runs exactly as before.

**Reuse:** it's a third caller of the suite's existing skip-with-a-reason, `test.skip(condition, reason)`. That's how
`maps.spec.ts` and `bus.spec.ts` already skip, and the reason is worded after `bus.spec.ts`'s.

**What answers "is it deployed?" is new,** because nothing existing could:
- **Feature detection** (the probe skips' way) can't tell "not yet deployed" from "deleted". That's the case a check must
  fail on.
- **The page can't name its commit,** because the banner is unstamped, #1306.
- **The tags can.** The quick tier in `run-local.sh` already reads them the same way.

**Rejected:**
- **Shape 2, feature detection alone,** for the reason above.
- **Shape 3, a dated allowance:** it's cheapest, but it depends on someone remembering to remove it, and a forgotten one
  hides a real failure, as #3539 warns.
