## 1. #3007: editing only a place's link marks the form changed

**The cause:**
- The link form (`place-link-form.component.html` in `libs/gisday/platform/ngx/common`) bound its boxes with
  `[(ngModel)]="value.label"` and `value.url`.
- That changed the link object the form had passed in, in place, and never called `onChange`.
- So the place form never became changed, and Escape left without asking.
- In the admin screens, a place is an Organization.

**The fix:**
- **In the shared base class:** `AbstractValueAccessorFormComponent` in `libs/ui-kits/ngx/forms` gets
  `setInternalProperty(key, value)`. It sets one field on a new object, and reports it the usual way. A value the form
  loads is still not reported, so #3001's rule holds.
- **In the link form:** its two boxes now use `[ngModel]` with `(ngModelChange)="setInternalProperty(...)"`.

**Red first, then green:**
- **A new `place-location-add-edit-form.component.spec.ts`:** on `development`, the URL-edit and label-edit tests failed,

## 2. #3009: the sponsor logo picker takes only what the server takes

**The cause:**
- The picker's `accept="image/jpeg,image/png"` was a hand-written copy of the server's rule.
- The form never checked the file. So an SVG chosen with "All files" was previewed, and refused only at Save.

**Where the rule lives now:** `libs/gisday/common/src/lib/upload-images.ts`, exported from the library. It holds:
- `UPLOAD_IMAGE_RULES`: formats `jpeg` and `png`, and `accept` `image/jpeg,image/png`;
- `uploadImageRefusal(label)`: the server's wording;
- `uploadImageProblem(file, label)`: the check the site uses.

**The server:** `refuseUnsafeImage` (`data-api/.../assets/image-upload.ts`) now takes its formats and message from that
file. It still judges a file by its content.

**The sponsor form:**
- The picker's `accept` comes from the shared rule.
- A file of another type resets the picker, and shows "The logo must be a JPEG or PNG image." under it, with the app's
  `.error` class. There's no new CSS.
- The saved logo stays in the preview.
