## 4. Why `?bldg=0468` opens too close on dev

**The cause:** the two basemaps number their zoom levels differently, and the app asks for a level number, not a scale.

**What the app does.** `MapService.selectFeatures` (`libs/maps/esri/src/lib/services/map/map.service.ts:1120`) zooms to a selection at
whatever level `computeZoomLevel` (`:1169`) returns:
- a selection up to 0.0001 across gets level 20;
- up to 0.004, level 19;
- up to 0.01, level 17.

That is a level **number**, and the view turns it into a scale using the basemap's own tiling scheme.

**What the two tiling schemes say** (each service's `tileInfo.lods`):

| Level | `TAMU_Basemap_20261008` (raster; production, and dev's raster entry) | `Hosted/VTBase3` (October vector; dev's default) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 1:591,657,528 | 1:295,828,764 |
| 17 | 1:4,514 | 1:2,257 |
| 18 | 1:2,257 | 1:1,128 |
| **19** | **1:1,128** | **1:564** |
| 20 | 1:564 | 1:282 |

The vector cache uses the 512-pixel tile scheme vector tiles are published with, so its level *n* is the raster's level *n + 1*. The
same "level 19" is twice as close.

**Measured, same link, same pane size:**
- both environments land at **zoom 19**, centre -96.33897, 30.61659 (the map probe's `framing`);
- production shows the whole of Evans Library ([capture](2026-10-26-143/bldg-0468-production-zoom19.jpg));
- dev shows a corner of it ([capture](2026-10-26-143/bldg-0468-dev-zoom19.jpg)).

**So report 142's "several levels closer" was wrong: it is exactly one level, a factor of two.** Its captures differed by more because
they were taken at different viewport sizes, as that report said.

**What follows from it:**
- **Every building link and search result on a map that opens on VTBase3 is affected.** Every other zoom number the app hard-codes is
  affected too: anything that passes `zoom:` to `goTo`, or sets a view's `zoom`. They all mean half the scale on VTBase3.
- **`trip-planner.service.ts:2333` has its own copy of `computeZoomLevel`**, with the same problem.
- **`AGGIEMAP_BASEMAP_MAX_SCALE` is a scale**, 282, so it is not affected. On VTBase3, 1:282 is level 20, which is where that cache's
  tiles stop.
- **A fix that does not depend on the basemap** is to ask for a scale (`goTo({ target, scale: 1128 })`) instead of a level number. The
  thresholds' units are a separate question: they read as degrees, and on these maps the geometry is in metres.
- **The GIS team** could republish VTBase3 on the 256-pixel scheme instead, but a scale-based fix covers any future cache too.

The cause is measured. The fix is not written or tried.
