## Questions for the director

**Q-a36-1: Graduation's two popup spellings.** The difference the issue describes is no longer inside this family.
- **Fall and spring** already share `GraduationColdLayerSources` (`graduation.definitions.ts`, #2530), which uses
  `'{attributes.name}'` and `'{attributes.description}'` throughout.
- **The `'attributes.name'` spelling** is in `graduation-summer.definitions.ts`. That's a separate map on its own service,
  not part of this batch.
- **I checked the services** (`?f=json`, production): `GraduationParking_view` layers 0, 1 and 3, and
  `Summer_Commencement_view` layer 1, all have `name` and `description`. So both spellings resolve.
  - The braced form goes through the template renderer, and gives a string.
  - The plain form reads the property, and gives the raw value.
- **The options:**
  - **(a)** Leave both. Each works, and changing one changes that map's output.
  - **(b)** Make summer's braced, to match the other graduation maps.
  - **(c)** Make the shared sources plain, which is the more common form here: 47 `name:` entries are plain and 31 braced.
- **I recommend (a)** for this pull request, which promises no change. If you want one spelling, (b) is a one-file change of
  its own.
