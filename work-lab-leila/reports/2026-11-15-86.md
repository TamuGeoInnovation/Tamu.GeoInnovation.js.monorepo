## What changed

**`tamu-gisc-action-menu`** (`ActionMenuComponent`, `ActionMenuItem`), in `ui-kits-ngx-layout`, exported from
`@tamu-gisc/ui-kits/ngx/layout`:
- **Inputs:** `label`, `icon`, `items`, `triggerClass` (so it takes the page's own `__form-action` or `selected-action`
  look), `opens` and `align`.
- **Output:** `chosen`, with the item's id.
- **Accessibility:** `aria-haspopup="menu"`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and a `role="menu"` list of `menuitem`s.
- **Keyboard:** arrows, Home and End move; Enter or Space chooses.
- **Closing:** Escape, a click outside, or Tab closes it, and focus returns to the button.
- **Escape is claimed by the menu,** so the details page doesn't also go back to its list.
- **Its buttons are `type="button"`,** so they never submit a form.

**Moved into the menu:**
- **The session admin page:** "Email these speakers", with Profile email and Session details. It replaces "Send profile
  emails to these speakers".
- **Research Competitions' selection bar:** "Email", with Profile email and Final reminder. Approve, Reject and Delete
  are unchanged.

**Not moved:**
- Speakers has a single email action, beside Copy and Delete.
- No other admin page has an email action.

**The session details email:**
- **The prefill,** from `sessionDetailsEmailTemplate` in `@tamu-gisc/gisday/common`, is written into the text so each line
  can be edited:
  - the name;
  - the day and time in Central;
  - the building and room, "Online", or "To be announced";
  - for hybrid or virtual sessions, the broadcast's link, password, dial-in number and meeting ID;
  - that the check-in QR code is shown at the session and scanned there;
  - what to bring, from the session's requirements;
  - the session page link.
- **Per speaker,** only `{first name}`, `{site}` and `{site address}` are filled in.
- **The composer:** it's the same `ReviewEmailModalComponent`, through a new `sendEditableEmail` that the profile email now
  uses too. The window also names any speaker with no email address.

**The API:** `POST session-emails/details`, and `…/details/preview`.
- **The guard:** both use `@Permissions(['update:speakers'])` with `JwtGuard` and `PermissionsGuard`, as the profile email
  does.
- **Sending:** in parallel, with `inParallel` and `EMAIL_LANES`.
- **The repeat guard:**
  - A speaker sent this session's details within `REPEAT_SEND_MINUTES` (10) is skipped, and named with the reason.
  - A failed send clears its record, so it can be retried at once.
- **Who it goes to:** the session's saved speakers.
