## #387: the lock-up doesn't reproduce, but the list can't scroll

**On dev** (`/map/m/sidebar/layers`), with Chromium's touch gestures (`Input.synthesizeScrollGesture`, `gestureSourceType: touch`): three
upward swipes on the Layers list at 375 × 380 and 375 × 812.

| | 375 × 380 | 375 × 812 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The page scrolled (`scrollingElement.scrollTop`) | **no, 0** (`scrollHeight` 404) | no, 0 |
| An element in the sidebar scrolled | **none has its own scrolling** | none |
| The last layer, *Visitor Parking* | **cut off at the bottom, unreachable** | in view |
| A tap on the map beside the panel | **closed it**: `/map/m/sidebar/layers` to `/map/m` | closed it |

**So the issue's "locked state" didn't happen** with touch. Dismissal was never scrolled away. **But the reason it didn't is the
real fault:** nothing scrolls. The list is clipped, not scrollable, so its end can't be reached on a short screen.
`387-375x380-scrolled.png` in this report's folder shows it. Several layers are struck through red there; that's the dev GIS server's
known missing services.

**Kept**, with a comment restating the claim. A headless touch gesture isn't a real phone, so iOS Safari may still behave as the
original report shows; the comment says so. **The fix is unchanged from 28 October:** give the sidebar's content its own scrolling area.

**Against the map:** `graduation-fall.definitions.ts` has two days today, `2025-12-17` (*"Wednesday, December 17th"*) and `2025-12-18`
(*"Thursday, December 18th"*). **Fall 2026 is also two days, a Friday and a Saturday.**

**As the proposal you asked for**, not changed in any file:

```ts
{ eventDate: '2026-12-11', value: '2026-12-11T06:00:00.000Z', label: 'Friday, December 11th' },
{ eventDate: '2026-12-12', value: '2026-12-12T06:00:00.000Z', label: 'Saturday, December 12th' }
```

**One thing to check while those are edited: the `value` times.**
- **Today's entries** use `T05:00:00.000Z`. That's midnight in **daylight** time (UTC-5).
- **December is standard time** (UTC-6), so midnight Central on 17 December 2025 is `06:00Z`, and `05:00Z` is **11 p.m. the evening
  before**.
- **The proposal uses `06:00Z`.** If the definitions use `05:00Z` everywhere on purpose, keep it; otherwise December's `value`s are an
  hour early.
- **Not traced:** I didn't trace how `value` is used, so I don't know whether the hour matters on screen.

appears to turn away the headless browser. **The map's `scheduleUrl` works for a visitor;** I read the schedule from the host. A smoke
