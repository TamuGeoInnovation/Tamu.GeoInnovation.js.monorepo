## 3. The fixes (no `!`, no casts)

**`SubmissionComponent` (11):**
- **Its fields say they can be `undefined`:** `formModel`, `file` and `file2`, their object URLs, `location`,
  `_trackLocation` and `_submissionGuid`. `_destroy$` is a `Subject<void>`.
- **`submitResponse()` reads what the upload needs once, before it starts:**
  - the position, which the location check just above already ensures for a valid form on an open season;
  - the photograph;
  - `formModel.season`.

  The upload then uses those three.

**`DeviceGuard` (4):**
- **An explicit `false`** where `canActivate` and `isDeviceAuthorized` fell through with `undefined`. The router already
  treated a result that isn't `true` as a refusal, so nothing changes.
- **Each redirect is read once** into a constant, so its truthiness check narrows it.
