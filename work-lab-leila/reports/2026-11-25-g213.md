## The fixes (no `!`, no casts, no caller changed)

- **8 inputs** that start as `undefined` say so: `label`, `giscAccordionContent`, the stepper's `template`, the tooltip's
  `width`, `height` and `position`, and `elementInsert`. The content replacer's private `Toggle.label`, which copies one,
  follows.
- **The tooltip (33 of the 45):**
  - its private positioning methods take `Box = Pick<DOMRect, 'top' | 'left' | 'width' | 'height'>`, the four sides
    every caller gives, where `Partial<DOMRect>` let each be `undefined`;
  - `calculateX` and `calculateY` each end their chain over three positions in an `else`, since the union is exhausted.
    That removes "used before assigned" and changes nothing;
  - its destroy subject is `Subject<void>`.
- **`required` where they always are,** each throwing, if ever missing, where the old code would have thrown:
  - the tooltip's `triggerElem` (`contentChild.required`): read only once the tooltip is open, and it opens from its
    trigger;
  - the accordion's `_container` (`viewChild.required`): `#content` is its template's root, never behind an `@if`;
  - the accordion header's input (`input.required`): all 9 uses bind it, `[giscAccordionHeader]="parent"`.
- **The sliding drawer's `_removeUnits` and `_getUnits`** still throw on a measurement with no number or no unit. They
  now say so (`No number in the CSS measurement '…'`), where they threw reading `null`.

**Behaviour:** nothing a page shows or does changes, so there's no red-first spec. Every change either only describes a
value, or keeps a throw where it was.
