## 3. The fix

`prop="BldgAbbrev"` → **`BldgAbbr`**, in the template's column and in the component's `columns` list (two places).

**Every other `BldgAbbrev`** is in the search sources (`libs/aggiemap/ngx/common/.../search-sources.ts`, and the gisday-competitions and
ues-operations environments). They query the **ArcGIS buildings service**, where the field really is called `BldgAbbrev`, so they are right and
unchanged. Only the directory reads the local JSON, which calls it `BldgAbbr`, as does the component's own interface and its search filter.
