## What changed

- **A new shared component, `tamu-gisc-polygon-editor`** in `libs/maps/esri`, exported from its index.
  - It takes `corners`, `center` and `zoom`, and emits `cornersChange`.
  - **Draw** starts a zone. Clicking the zone lets you drag a corner or the whole zone. **Clear** removes it.
- **Map to box:** a change on the map is written to the box as `latitude, longitude` lines, to 6 decimals. It marks the
  form changed, so Escape asks before leaving.
- **Box to map:**
  - Typed corners are read with the save's own parser, and drawn.
  - While a line is half typed, the map keeps the last zone it could read.
- **Unchanged:** the box stays, and is still what's saved. There's no API or schema change.
- **Loaded later:** the map is in an `@defer (on immediate)` block, so ArcGIS loads after the form has drawn.
- **The default view:**
  - With corners, the map frames the zone. Otherwise it shows campus.
  - **A venue has no point of its own:** `event_location` has none, and its place has only an address. So "the venue's
    own point" can't be used.
