## 2. The upgrade

- **`chart.js` `^2.9.3` → `~4.5.1`.** **I chose 4.5.1, not the latest 4.6.1**, because 4.6.1 was published on 22 October, two days ago. The tilde
  keeps the lock off it; `^4.5.1` would have locked 4.6.1.
- **`@types/chart.js` dropped:** 4 ships its own types. The container's configuration types are now chart.js 4's `ChartType`, `ChartData` and
  `ChartOptions` (`IChartConfigurationOptions` is `ChartOptions`), replacing 2's `Chart.*` namespace.
- **Register only what is used:** each chart component registers its own type's pieces, in its own file. The bar chart registers
  `BarController`, `BarElement`, `CategoryScale` and `LinearScale`; line, pie and doughnut register theirs. The container registers `Legend`,
  `Tooltip` and `Title`, which all of them use. So an app that imports only the bar chart bundles only the bar chart.
- **`chartjs-plugin-colorschemes` dropped**: it supports chart.js `>= 2.5.0 < 3` only. It coloured the chart with its default palette, since no
  scheme is set anywhere. **Its palette (ColorBrewer Paired, 12) and its rules, read from its source, are now `applyChartColors`** in the
  container:
  - a bar gets the colour as its fill;
  - a line, radar or scatter gets it as border and points, with a 50% fill;
  - a pie, doughnut or polar area gets one colour per slice;
  - **never over a colour the caller set**.

  It has four new specs.
- **A live chart's data is updated through `chart.data`**, as 4 expects (typed fix: `chart.config` is a union in 4's types).
- **The lock**, by batch 41's method, key by key:
  - `chart.js` 2.9.4 → 4.5.1, and `@kurkle/color` 0.3.4 added (4's colour library);
  - **removed**: `chartjs-plugin-colorschemes`, `@types/chart.js`, `chartjs-color`, `chartjs-color-string` and its nested `color-convert` and
    `color-name`, and **`moment` 2.31.0**, which only chart.js 2 pulled in (nothing in the repository imports it).
