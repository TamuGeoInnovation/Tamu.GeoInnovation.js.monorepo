## 1. #2822: the 3D test no longer waits on a basemap it cannot click

Commit `b919ce7b5` changes `test/smoke/aggiemap/perspective.spec.ts`, in "3D is a globe with its 3D buildings on every campus basemap offered here". It makes three changes:

- **A basemap the gallery marks `aria-disabled` in 3D is not clicked.** The test asserts its `unavailable` class instead. A State Plane basemap that 3D refuses without showing it as unavailable still fails, and the skip takes milliseconds.
- **Each click in 3D has a 30-second timeout.** A click that cannot land now fails in seconds instead of 15 minutes.
- **Back in 2D, the test compares the scale, not the zoom.** Once the test got past the click, it reached an assertion it had never reached on dev, and that assertion was wrong.
  - Zoom is counted in each basemap's own levels, and a vector tile basemap counts one fewer than a raster one at the same scale. VTBase3's level 16 and the raster's 17 are both 1:4,514.
  - So a test ending on a raster basemap read 17 against 16, with the map exactly where it started.
  - My first version of the fix failed on exactly this, three times. The scale is read from the `<arcgis-map>` element's view, as `building-clicks.spec.ts` already does.
