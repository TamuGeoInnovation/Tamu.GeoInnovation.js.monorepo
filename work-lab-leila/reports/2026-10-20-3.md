## Findings

1. **The Team Bus Parking area is drawn on the build, and hidden under Indoor Track Building.** The fix works: on
   production the layer's unique-value renderer keys `type` on "Team Drop Off" and "Team Bus Parking", its one
   feature holds `type: SPORTS`, and nothing draws. On the build it is a simple renderer, the layer view holds the
   feature, and it draws solid magenta (`[255,0,197]`). But the polygon is the Indoor Track building's footprint,
   and the map orders Team Bus Parking (layer 11) **below** Indoor Track Building (layer 13). With every layer on
   as the map opens, the gold building covers it completely. So the *What to test on dev* row home drafted, "the
   magenta Team Bus Parking area", will not be seen by a tester who just opens the map. I kept home's row and
   wording and took the pair with the building switched off. Worth deciding before the PR whether the row should
   say "switch off Indoor Track Building", or whether the layer order is the real bug.
2. **The build's Team Bus Parking legend loses its "Team Drop Off" row.** Production lists two rows under Team Bus
   Parking, Team Drop Off (blue) and Team Bus Parking (magenta); the build lists only Team Bus Parking, as a simple
   renderer would. Probably right, because no feature holds "Team Drop Off", but it is a visible change the notes
   do not mention.
3. **A `?feature=` link on the #1992 build opens "Selected Buildings" with a raw template.** Opening
   `/parking/indoor-track-parking/map/d?feature=indoor-track-parking-team-bus:1204` selected the area and showed a
   popup titled "Selected Buildings" whose body was the literal text `{attributes.sp_sh_notes}`. The title is
   #1970 (PR 2016); the unfilled template may be the same cause or a second one. Not investigated.
4. **Women's Basketball opens a "This event has passed" dialog** over the map, on production and on the build. The
   captures close it with its OK button first. The first production capture had it, and was retaken.
