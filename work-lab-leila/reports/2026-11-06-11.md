  - **Gene Stallings Blvd Garage** (1544), centroid -96.343424, 30.611518.

  Both environments return identical geometry for them.

**Clicking by map coordinates, re-read per load:**
- **Where:** at the click, the target's point goes through `view.toScreen`, and `document.elementFromPoint` checks what's there.
- **No view yet** (at 0 s, and at 1 s in some loads): the harness clicked where the target sits once framed, which is where a visitor
  would click. Those are counted, as "no view yet".
- **The app's loading overlay** (`div.loader`) still covers the map at 1 to 2 s. Those clicks were made anyway, as a visitor's would

- **No load was unusable:** no target ever fell outside the visible map.

**What was recorded in the page itself, every 50 to 100 ms:** when the view first reported `ready`, when the probe first said `ready:
true`, when the probe's `drawing` first went false after that, and every change of either.

**Once a popup opens, it takes a while:**

A visitor who sees nothing for two seconds and clicks again is plausible. I didn't measure double clicks.

### Why, measured

- **The code:** the popup comes only from `MapService`'s click handler. It's `view.hitTest(e)` and nothing else
  (`libs/maps/esri/src/lib/services/map/map.service.ts:222-240`): no query as a fallback and no retry.
- **The test:** I re-ran 24 dev loads, at 4, 8 and 12 s after load and after drawing finished, and before each click called
  `view.hitTest` at the same point myself.

| `hitTest` at the click included `buildings-layer` | popup opened | no popup |
| --- | --- | --- |
| yes | **14** | 0 |
| no (only the basemap's vector tile layer, or nothing) | 1 | **9** |

- **The one exception:** in one load at 12 s, my hit test, a moment before the click, found only the basemap, and the click itself still
  opened the popup. The layer view came up in between.
- **In every miss, `buildings-layer`'s layer view was still `updating`.** A hit starts to show up while it's still updating, and
  always once it has stopped.
- **So the director's account in #2707 is right for the popup:** before the buildings layer view holds the graphics under the point,
  the hit test is empty, and the click is thrown away without an error.

**The gaps:**
- **from the load event to `ready`:** about 2.5 s;

  came up later than ILSB in 6 of 6 pairs;

**What the page looks like in the gap:** not finished. Drawing ends after the first working click, so a page that "looks" finished is
past the window by then.

**`ready` is wrong in the direction that hurts the suite:**
- **It's true before a click works.** `popup.spec.ts` survives only because it also waits for `drawing === false`.
- **`map-coordinates.spec.ts` waits for `waitForSettledFraming`, which is `ready` plus a steady framing.** That's the bucket that
  opened 0 of 20 popups here. Its directions test passes because routing doesn't need the layer view, not because the wait is enough.

**#2707's observation, `ready: false` on a finished map, didn't reproduce:**
- **Loads:** 12 quiet loads watched for 60 s each, and the `ready` log of every load in the sets above.
- **What was different:** #2707 saw it after switching sidebar panels and after a reload of `/map/d/trip`. I loaded `/map/d/trip`
  fresh 280 times on dev and never switched panels.

## Questions for the director (aggiemap)

1. **What would fix the popup?** Options:
   - **(a)** hold the click until `buildings-layer`'s layer view stops updating, then hit-test;
   - **(b)** when the hit test comes back empty, fall back to a query of the layers at the point, as routing already does;
   - **(c)** make the probe's `ready` wait for the layer views, not just the layers, so the suite's waits mean "clickable".

   **Recommended:** (b) for visitors, since it's the path that already works in routing, and (c) for the suite. They're separate
   issues. (a) makes a click feel slow.
2. **The Nature Rx marker in routing:** should a click on a named point layer give the stop that name? **Recommended:** yes. Let the
   handler try the clicked feature's own layer before `buildings-layer`, and fall back to coordinates only on bare ground. It's a
   small, separate issue from #2707.
3. **The checks to add**, which #2707 says wait for this rate. **Recommended:**
   - a popup check that clicks by `view.toScreen` and asserts a popup **only once a hit test includes the target's layer**. As written
     today it would fail at settled framing, which is the bug.
   - after the fix, the same check run at the settled bucket, where today it gets 0 of 20 on dev and 1 of 20 on production.
   - a routing check that clicks a Nature Rx marker and expects its name, once question 2 is decided.
4. **`ready: false` on a drawn map:** want me to measure the panel-switching path that #2707 saw it on? Switch sidebar panels after
   load, then log `ready` and `drawing`, 20 times. **Recommended:** yes, as a small batch. It's the only part of #2707 this batch
   couldn't reproduce.
