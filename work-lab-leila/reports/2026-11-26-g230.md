## What kubernetes-config needs (step 6), for the maintainer

I have not looked at or edited `kubernetes-config`.

1. **An image:** build `gisday-pptx-preview` with `--configuration=production`, then `docker build` in
   `dist/apps/gisday-pptx-preview` (the build copies the Dockerfile there). Name it like the API's, for example
   `gisday-pptx-preview`. Nothing in this repository's CI builds or pushes it yet; whatever builds the API's image needs
   the same step for this one.
2. **A Deployment** beside the API's: one replica is enough; container port 8080; liveness and readiness on
   `GET /health`; it runs as the image's `node` user; no volumes and no secrets. Memory: a conversion is one LibreOffice
   process. I did not measure its peak, so a limit of about 1 GiB is a guess to check.
3. **A Service** (ClusterIP, port 8080), and a NetworkPolicy so only the API's pods reach it: the batch asks that only the
   API can reach it, and the service has no authentication of its own.
4. **On the API:** `PPTX_PREVIEW_URL=http://<service name>:8080`. Until it is set, production behaves exactly as today.
5. Optional: `MAX_BYTES` and `TIMEOUT_MS` on the service (see Q-g230-2).

**The image is larger than the laptop measured.** Docker reports `gisday-pptx-preview:local` as 1.76 GB, against 244 MB
for `node:24.22.3-alpine`; the `apk add` layer alone is 1.09 GB on this machine. The laptop's +426 MB on 23 November was
probably a compressed size; I have not checked that against its report.
