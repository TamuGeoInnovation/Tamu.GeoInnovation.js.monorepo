## The four questions in the batch, answered

### 1. Which maps: measured, then chosen

**I surveyed all 81 maps the crawl lists on dev first** (every popup layer, and what is clickable in view):

- **13 are builder maps.** They need their recorded links, which `servedRoutes` gives.
- **15 of the 68 showed nothing clickable at all.** On 14 past events and soccer parking, the "event has passed" notice
  covers the map until it is closed. **The spec now closes it with Escape**, as a visitor would.

**What each run clicks:**

| Run | Maps | Measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Release, `run-local.sh`'s default | `RELEASE_MAPS`: `/map/d`, AVP parking, NSC parking, Football Tailgating, Galveston, the construction map: one per section | **1.8 minutes** on dev, 7 tests, 49 clicks, none missed |
| The nightly, and `full` | every map served, one route each (`servedRoutes`, moved from `framing.spec.ts` into `framing.ts`, unchanged) | **25 minutes** on production with no retries (77 tests), **50** on dev with retries (84) |

**Why this split:**

- At a release, every map would add 25 to 50 minutes to a 30-minute tier. These tests are `@heavy`, two at a time.
- The nightly is where a map nobody touched breaks, and nobody waits on it.

The release set is checked against what the environment serves, so a renamed or retired map fails rather than
dropping out.

### 2. Not only buildings: yes, in this spec

**The same method works for any polygon layer.** Every polygon layer that opens a popup is clicked: up to 6 buildings and
2 features of each other layer per map, 14 at most. That covers lots, campus property, an event's own areas.

- **A click counts when the popup shows one of the feature's names:** its layer's `popupData.name`, resolved as
  `popup.service.ts` resolves it (a template, a path such as `attributes.name`, or a field), or its own name fields.
  - **Why not one field:** Galveston's popup titles its buildings from the campus configuration, which a page cannot
    read.
- **A point another popup layer answers is not used.** Before this rule, most misses across all maps on dev were a
  bike rack, a bus stop or an event's own lot rightly answering a click on a building or campus lot.
- **Point and line layers are not clicked targets**, because a marker is reached by its symbol, not its geometry.
  They are asked as "others" in the rule above.

### 3. A rate, not a pass

**Each map passes at 90%:** one miss in fourteen passes, two do not.

- Every click is reported with its time and the framing it was made at: "13 of 14 clicks opened their own popup (zoom
  17, ...)".
- **A map with nothing in view to click fails.** That's the bus.spec lesson.
- Failed buildings queries still fail the map on their own, as the three-map version did (#2702).

### 4. The framing is read, not assumed

Every point comes from `view.toScreen` on the live view, and the framing is printed with each result.
