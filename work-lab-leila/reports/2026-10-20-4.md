## 2. #1969: reproduced, the cause shown, and a way to make it fail every time

All against dev (`https://dev.aggiemap.tamu.edu`, serving **`main-IORMDVQQ.js`**), `lot-links.spec.ts` from
`development` at `2c1651c9b`, retries off.

### How often, and does load change it?

**Yes.** Counting only the #1969 symptom (the popup shares the main map's `/?lot=Lot 100j`, not the event
map's own feature):

| Run | Workers | Machine | Visitor Parking | Motorcycle Parking | Together |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 1 | quiet | 0 / 40 | 0 / 40 | **0%** |
| 1 | 6 | quiet | 1 / 20 | 1 / 20 | **5%** |
| 3 | 6 | all 24 CPUs busy (a separate container running 24 busy loops) | 5 / 40 | 5 / 40 | **12.5%** |
| 2 | 24 | quiet | 4 / 40 | 7 / 40 | **13.8%** |

It is not specific to one map: both maps show it at the same rate. And it is load, not worker count: six workers
on a saturated machine fail as often as twenty-four on a quiet one.

**Motorcycle Parking also failed almost every run on a second check, which is #1970, not this.** Every run where
the right lot opened then failed `lot-links.spec.ts:147`, "opened a popup headed by the selection layer"
(Selected Buildings): 19 of 20, 33 of 40, 35 of 40, 40 of 40. Its fix is in PR 2016, which is not on dev. Those
failures come after the #1969 check has passed, so they are counted out above.

### What is actually happening

Reading the popup the moment it appears, rather than when the map says ready as the spec does:

| Visitor Parking's own service delayed by | Popup when it first appears | When the map probe says `ready` | 5 s after `ready` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| nothing | main map's lot, 3 of 4 | own layer, 4 of 4 | own layer |
| 4 s | main map's lot, 4 of 4 | **main map's lot, 4 of 4** | own layer |
| 10 s | main map's lot, 4 of 4 | **main map's lot, 4 of 4** | **main map's lot, 4 of 4** |

So **the main map's lot opens first almost every time**, and is replaced when the event map's own layer answers.
The map reports `ready` without waiting for that answer. When the event layer's service is slow, through load or
the network, `ready` arrives first. Both the spec and a person see the main map's lot. The issue's hypothesis is
right, with one addition: it is not only that the lookup *can* fall through. It does fall through first on nearly
every load, and the fix is quietly replacing a wrong popup nearly every time.

### A red-first test

**Delay the event map's own parking service and it fails every time.** With Playwright,
`page.route((url) => url.href.includes('TS_Events/VisitorParking/MapServer'), …)` waiting 4 s before
`route.continue()`, and the popup read when the probe says `ready` (as `openedPopup` in `lot-links.spec.ts` does),
4 of 4 open the main map's Lot 100j. No delay: 0 of 4. The same works for Motorcycle Parking with
`TS_Events/MotorcycleParking/MapServer`. Nothing was fixed or committed. The experiment is a script outside the
repository.
