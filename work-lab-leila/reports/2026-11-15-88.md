- **The choice is checked before anything is sent** on all five composer routes:
  - `speaker-edits/send`;

  - `session-emails/details`;
  - `user-submissions/review`;
  - `user-submissions/remind`.

**The rules:**
- **Allowed:** the admin's **own** address, the site's `MAILROOM_FROM`, or an entry in the new **`MAILROOM_SENDERS`**. All
  must be tamu.edu, except `MAILROOM_FROM`.
- **The own address** comes from the token's email claim, or else from the Auth0 account the token names, never from the
  request.
- **A non-tamu.edu entry in `MAILROOM_SENDERS`** is ignored, with one warning in the log, and a send as it gets a 400.
- **Anything else** gets a 400 with a reason.

**What From and Reply-To become:** the mailroom passes both to the relay as they are.

| Choice | From | Reply-To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Own address | `"<Name> (<Site>)" <own>` | the own address |
| A shared address | `"<Site>" <shared>` | the shared address |
| The site's address, or no choice sent | as before | `MAILROOM_TO`, as before |

**The window:**
- The box is at the top, with the own address chosen by default.
- The preview shows "From: … Replies to: …".
- If the own address can't be offered, the window says why.
