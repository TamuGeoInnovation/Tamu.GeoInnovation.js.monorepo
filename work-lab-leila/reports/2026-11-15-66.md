    paper and cartography competitions. Poster closes …".
- **Inside:** the competitions' checkboxes, then a deadlines row for each ticked competition. The rows follow the boxes.
- **One Save** sends both the competitions and the deadlines.
  - **Checked first:** both are checked in the browser first. If either fails, nothing is sent, and the page says why.
  - **If the server refuses one,** the message names it, for example "The competitions were saved. The deadlines were
    not: …".

- **The panel stays open after a save,** so its message can be read. It opens or closes by itself only when another
  season is chosen.
- **The files:**

  - `season-competitions` is folded into it and deleted.
  - There's no API or schema change.
  - There's no new CSS: the stylesheet is the deadlines panel's, with its classes renamed.
- **Reused:**
  - the accordion directives;
