## 2. #2702's check: `test/2702-basemap-requests`, head `fd5c547c62f7af3fca393c88287825683d7c2728`

**#2702 stays open for the GIS team**, as you said. So the commit says `Part of #2702`, and the PR needs an issue of its
own for its `Closes` line. I'd suggest Task, `testing`, Texas A&M Aggiemap, Low.

**What I found first: the failures are HTTP 200.**
- A failed query answers **200** with `{"error":{"code":500,"message":"Error performing query operation"}}` in the body:
  3 of 193 direct count queries, all 200.
- **That's why nothing in the suite sees it.** `maps.spec.ts` collects "server errors" by status (`>= 500`), so it
  passes them.
- My earlier reports said "500" from the body's code. I should have said the HTTP status was 200.

**The measurement the threshold rests on** (dev, 15 plain loads of `/map` at 1280 x 950):
- **7 of 540 queries failed (1.30%)**, 36 queries a load, none of them HTTP 5xx.
- 7 loads lost one query and **none lost two**.
- That agrees with report 10's 1.4 to 1.6% and report 12's 5 of 10 loads.

**The threshold: more than 4% across ten loads (360 queries).** The binomial at the measured rate:

| Rule | Fails at today's 1.5% | at 5% | at 8% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No load loses more than one query (your example) | **about 2 runs in 3** | - | - |
| More than 3% across ten loads | about 1 run in 46 | 97% | 100% |
| **More than 4% across ten loads** (chosen) | **about 1 run in 2,300** | 80% | 100% |

**The rule your batch suggested would be noise.** At 1.5%, one load in ten loses two queries, so ten loads trip it two
runs in three. 3% would fail about once a month across two nightly environments. 4% is quiet: about once in three years
of nightly runs at today's rate.

**So say this plainly to the maintainer: it doesn't fail on the fault as it is today.** It catches the service getting
markedly worse (80% of runs at 5%, every run at 8%). It also logs the measured rate on every run, as
`[smoke] basemap queries (#2702): 6 of 360 failed (1.67%) across 10 loads; per load: 1/36 1/36 0/36 ...`, and as an
annotation in the HTML report. So the GIS team's fix, or a slow drift, can be read from any run without measuring again.
A check that fails on today's 1.5% can't be quiet, and you said to bring that back rather than ship a flapping test, so
this is the meaningful half.

**Easy to change:**
- `AGGIEMAP_SMOKE_BASEMAP_LOADS` (default 10) and `AGGIEMAP_SMOKE_BASEMAP_MAX_FAILED_RATE` (default 0.04).
- `run-local.sh` now passes both into its container, as it does `AGGIEMAP_SMOKE_BLANK_LOADS`.
- The defaults are constants at the top of the spec, with the table above in its comment.

**Where it lives: a spec of its own**, `basemap-requests.spec.ts`, one `@heavy` test of about 1.5 minutes.
- **Why not `maps.spec.ts`:** I looked there first, since it already watches responses. It loads each map once, and a
  rate needs several loads of the same page. Adding ten loads to one of its per-map tests would have hidden a different
  kind of check inside it.
- **Its own `blank-map.spec.ts`** is the closest pattern (repeated plain loads, an environment variable for the count,
  `@heavy`), and I followed it.
- **It also fails if a load sends fewer than 10 basemap queries**, because then the map no longer reads the basemap as
  the check assumes and the rate means nothing.

**One thing I caught while proving it:**
- A context made by hand gets Playwright's default 1280 x 720 window, and at that size a load sends **24** queries, not
  36. At 240 queries the 4% ceiling fails about one run in 250, ten times noisier.
- So the spec sets the window to 1280 x 950 explicitly, and says why.
