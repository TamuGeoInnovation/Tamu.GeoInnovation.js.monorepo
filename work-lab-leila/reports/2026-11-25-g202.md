## Why not SWC, measured

Every `@Column`'s `design:type` was compared, as `tsc` writes it for the whole program and as each transform writes it,
across the platform's 218 columns.

| Transform | Columns whose `design:type` differs from `tsc` | Of those, with no explicit type (so TypeORM uses it) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| SWC (`unplugin-swc` 2.0.0, the decided route) | 35 | 15 |
| Vite 8's transform, as configured | 35 | 15 |
| **Vite 8's transform, `strictNullChecks: false`** | 17 | **4** |

**Why the two differ from `tsc`:**
- **Most of the 35 are `string | null` or `Date | null`.** `tsc` writes `String` or `Date` under `strict: false`; a
  one-file compiler that assumes strict null checks writes `Object`. Vite's transform has an option for that, and SWC
  doesn't.
- **The four left are:**
  - `Sponsor.logoSize`, a type alias (even SWC writes `typeof SponsorLogoSize === "undefined" ? Object : …`);
  - `Submission.format`, `submissionType` and `classificationType`, each an enum or a string.
- **The fix for those four:** each now declares `type: 'nvarchar', length: 255`.
  - **Why that changes nothing:** it's exactly what TypeORM made of `String`, and what `AddSponsorLogoSize1793810744009`
    and `InitialSchema1791325939619` created.

**What I tried, in order:**
1. **SWC, as decided:** 66 tests failed in `gisday-platform-data-api` and 3 in `gisday-competitions-data-api`. I installed it
   by the rules: 2.0.0 was 64 days old, it has no install script, and its six new dependencies were all over 7 days old
   with none either. Clean `npm ci` passed.
2. **The four columns given their type:** SWC still failed, on `SpeakerEmailSend.eventGuid` (`string | null`).
3. **Vite's transform with `strictNullChecks: false` and the four columns:** 1,235 of 1,238 passed. The three left were the
   two specs below.

**So the branch:**
- **Packages:** removes `unplugin-swc` again, leaving `package.json` and the lock as on `development`.
- **The preset:** gives `vitest.preset.mts` a `decoratorMetadata` option. Each of the three projects takes
  `{ angular: false, environment: 'node', decoratorMetadata: true }`.

Both routes are in the branch's history: `dc15268d5` is the SWC attempt, and `5a4aca47b` replaces it.

**Proof the metadata is right, not only that tests pass:**
- **Two specs, with metadata emission switched off:**
  - `speaker.controller.spec.ts`, a controller injected by type;
  - `base-provider.queries.spec.ts`, TypeORM building every entity's columns.

  Both fail as they load: `ColumnTypeUndefinedError: Column type for OldCompetitionEntity#id is not defined and cannot be
  guessed. Make sure you have turned on an "emitDecoratorMetadata"`.
- **With it on:** 11 of 11 pass.
- **The whole comparison above** was against `tsc`'s own emit.
