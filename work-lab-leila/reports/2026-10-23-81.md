## 2. The upgrade

- **`^20.1.0` → `^25.1.0`.** 25's peers are Angular `20.x || 21.x || 22.x`.
- **The lock**, by batch 41's method, key by key: **only `node_modules/@swimlane/ngx-datatable` 20.1.0 → 25.1.0**, and the root's range.
- **The changelog's breaking changes against our two uses:**
  - Angular 19 and earlier dropped: we are on 22.
  - The `sort` and `select` outputs removed: **neither table uses them**. The review list uses `(activate)`, the directory `[sorts]`.
  - The visibility observer removed (`ResizeObserver` instead): nothing to change.
  - Every input we bind still exists in 25.1.0's types: `scrollbarV`, `scrollbarH`, `virtualization`, `loadingIndicator`, `reorderable`,
    `columnMode`, `sorts`, `TableColumn`, `NgxDatatableModule`.
- **What the first build found:**
  - **`offset` is now a model signal**: `TS2540: Cannot assign to 'offset'` in the directory, which resets the table after a search. Now
    `table.offset.set(0)`.
- **What the first after screenshots found.** 25 lays the table out with a CSS grid, and our styles were written for 20's markup:

| Seen | Cause | Fix |

| every row 41 px, not 40 | the row wrapper is a `content-box` grid of `rowHeight`; our 1px `border-bottom` added to it | the line is an inset `box-shadow` |
