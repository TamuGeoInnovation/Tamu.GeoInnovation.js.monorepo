**The 7 fixes,** with no `!` and no casts:
- **`seasonYearFromRoute`** (`season.service.ts`) walks `route.firstChild`, which is `null` at the end, so the loop
  variable says `ActivatedRouteSnapshot | null`.
- **`FormService.getFormForSeason`** now says `Observable<CompetitionForm | null | undefined>`: that's what
  `pluck('form')` yields, since `CompetitionSeason.form` is optional and nullable. **Nothing calls it**, so no caller
  changes.
- **`SubmissionService`'s four optional `seasonGuid` params** are typed `Record<string, string>`. `HttpClient`'s
  `params` accepts that, where the ternary's `{}` branch had made the union unassignable. That fixes 5 errors, two of
  them on the zip download's overload.

**The 8 left, in imported libraries outside GIS Day:**
- **`common-ngx-ui-notification` (4):** `input<Notification>(undefined)` and `input<'left' | …>(undefined)`, a timer
  field typed from its first value, and a `find` assigned to a non-optional type.
- **`ui-kits-ngx-layout-modal` (4):** `viewChild`s read as always set. That library is already strict, and is listed
  at 87 in g210's known list.
- **Why I left them:** fixing them means widening shared inputs that other apps pass, so they belong to that library's
  own strict step (as g212 does for `ui-kits-ngx-forms`), not to this one.

**The spec configurations** of both libraries still type-check with 0 errors. I didn't turn the flag on there, as the
