**The About test checks the submenu's template, not its title.** `TileTitleComponent` reads the title with `innerText`, which jsdom
doesn't implement, so the title is undefined in a unit test. The browser shows the title as before.

## The fix

| File | What |
| --- | --- |
| `tile.component.ts` | **The click handler:** a click anywhere on a tile with a link follows it, unless the click was on the link's own element, which follows it itself (so a click on the word navigates once). A tile with a submenu opens it, as before. A tile with neither (Login) passes a click elsewhere on the tile to its title, so the page's own handler runs. **Host attributes:** a link tile is `role="link"` and a submenu tile `role="button"` with `aria-haspopup`, each with `tabindex="0"`. **Keys:** Enter follows a link; Enter or Space opens a submenu. A key on a title the page made focusable is left to the page. A comment says why (#2588) |
| `tile.component.scss` | `cursor: pointer` and a 3 px `#1a3f73` focus ring (`:focus-visible`) on a tile that acts |
| `tile-link.directive.ts` | `follow()` and `contains()`, so the tile can follow the link; the directive's own click calls `follow()`, so nothing else changes |
| `tile-title.component.ts` | `element`, the title's own element |
