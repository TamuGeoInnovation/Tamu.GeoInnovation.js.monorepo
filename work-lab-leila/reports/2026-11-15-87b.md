## One departure from the brief: the uploader isn't `Asset.speaker` (question 1)

**Why not:** `Asset.speaker` is the other side of `Speaker.images`, the speaker's **photo**. Storing slides there would
mean:
- the public speaker endpoints, which load `images`, would publish each slide's path;
- the avatar and the edit page read `images[0]`, so a slide could show as a photo;
- copying a season copies `images`, so the slides would be copied too;
- replacing a photo saves `images: [photo]`, which would detach the slides from their speaker.

**So there's a separate relation,** `Asset.uploadedBy` (`assets.uploadedByGuid`, a foreign key to `speakers`, `ON DELETE
SET NULL`). The migration changed because of it.
