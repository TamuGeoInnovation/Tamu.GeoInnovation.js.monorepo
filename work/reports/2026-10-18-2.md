### Why the tag is held

The maintainer knew of a legend fault before this run and said the legend check would catch it. **It did
not fail**, on the first attempt or at all, and neither did the other two legend tests.

A new check that passes over a known fault is worse than no check: every release after this one would be
cleared on it. Three explanations, and this run cannot separate them — the fault is not what the check
tests (it checks that switching a layer off and on is followed by both the Layers list and the legend);
the check has a hole; or the fault needs a map or layer the check does not use.

The maintainer chose to hold the tag (decision 1A, put to him as the run finished) and **the home session
is investigating why the check did not fail**. Nothing further is needed from this machine until that
comes back.
