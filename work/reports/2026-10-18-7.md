### Explained by this release

1. **The locate button, on every map.** Production draws Esri 4.x's navigation arrow; Release-482 draws the 5.x
   control, an "A" in a circle, a few pixels higher, and the compass below it shifts. #1858, #1892, #1893, #1900.
   It accounts for the ~0.19% that nearly every shot shows left of x=70.
2. **Dense point layers re-render a pixel out.** `/parking/sustainable-transportation` differs by 12% of its
   pixels and is indistinguishable by eye — several hundred bike-rack pins each a pixel from where 4.34 put them.
   Same for the gameday parking and move-in sets. The ArcGIS 4.34 to 5.1 step.
3. **`/map` and the campus maps are unchanged in the body** — 0.01% each, all of it the control strip. Since
   #1911 changed what the campus maps load, that is the result worth having.

throwaway scripts were run in the Playwright container against the two sets: one measuring the changed-pixel

bodies line up (it does not: they are at the same position, so nothing panned). Neither was committed, and the
worktree is clean.
